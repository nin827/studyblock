# Applied scenarios (Mathematician)

Expert+ questions for the **Mathematician** role. Each one is a single scenario with three or four linked answers, and each answer uses a different skill: statistics, probability, logarithms, calculus or the maths of money. A later part often builds on an earlier one, so work in order. The calculator is on, and the numbers from the prompt are already in it where that helps.

Every part says how to round. If you reuse a rounded earlier answer, the later answer still comes out the same.

## Quality-control report

You check a production line. Either summarise a sample (mean, population standard deviation, the z-score of a new reading) and decide whether to flag it, or estimate a defect rate, use it in a binomial model and decide whether a customer's target is met.

## Pricing insurance risk

Actuarial work. Use a life table given in the question to find survival and death probabilities, then the expected present value of a life policy and its premium with a loading. Or combine how often claims happen with how big they are, then discount the expected losses over several years.

## Growth and decay model

Fit an exponential model to two readings, use logarithms to find when it reaches a target, and differentiate to find the growth rate at a moment. Or start from a half-life: find the decay constant, the amount left, the time to fall to a percentage and the rate of decay.

## Checking a derivation

A colleague's worked derivation has exactly one wrong line: solving an equation with a fraction, finding a tangent, or computing a variance. Find the wrong step, give the number that line should end with, and give the correct final answer.
