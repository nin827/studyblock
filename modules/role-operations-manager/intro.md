# Applied scenarios (Operations Manager)

Expert+ questions for the **Operations Manager** role. Each one is a single scenario with several linked answers, and each answer uses a different skill: speed and time, time zones, rounding up to whole trucks or workers, percentages, dates or stock control. A later part usually builds on an earlier one, so work in order. The calculator is on, and the numbers from the prompt are already in it where that helps.

Every part says how to round, and counts (boxes, pallets, trucks, workers) always round **up**. Time zones are fixed offsets with no daylight saving, money is in a generic `$`, and clock times are 24-hour.

## Routes and arrival times

A truck drives a stated distance at a stated speed. Find the driving time, add the break rule, and give the arrival time in the customer's time zone. At Expert you decide whether it is on time. At Expert+ you work backwards from the deadline to the latest departure.

## Loads and trucks

Units go into boxes, boxes onto pallets, pallets onto trucks, and every step rounds up. At Expert+ a truck has both a space limit and a weight limit, and you find which one binds and whether the fleet is enough.

## Staffing and labour cost

Work out what one worker makes in a shift, how many workers a day's demand needs, and what that crew costs. At Expert+ the crew is too small for a week's demand, so you size the gap and compare hiring with overtime.

## Stock and reorder dates

A reorder point is the usage during the lead time plus safety stock. Find how long until the count reaches it, and the calendar date the order goes out. At Expert+ the daily usage is hidden in the reorder point, and you also check the safety stock against a policy.
