# Continents: how to solve

## General
- Picture the map: find the country's neighbours, then the continent they share.
- Use seven continents: Africa, Antarctica, Asia, Europe, North America, Oceania (Australia and the Pacific), South America.
- Typed answers ignore capitals and extra spaces, but spelling counts. For oceans, type the name only when the question says "Leave out the word 'Ocean'".

## Africa and Europe
1. Locate the country: south of the Mediterranean or Sahara points to **Africa**; north of it points to **Europe**.
2. Use neighbours: if it borders Kenya, Ghana or Nigeria it is African; if it borders Germany, Romania or Austria it is European.
3. Islands count with the nearest mainland: Madagascar (off East Africa) is African; Iceland (North Atlantic) is European.
4. Landlocked small states belong to their surroundings: Burkina Faso and Malawi are African; Liechtenstein and Moldova are European.

Watch out: Egypt and Morocco face Europe across the sea but are African.

## Asia and Oceania
1. Mainland or near the Asian coast → **Asia** (Mongolia, Nepal, Bhutan, Laos, Vietnam).
2. Australia, New Zealand and the Pacific islands → **Oceania** (Fiji, Samoa, Vanuatu, Papua New Guinea).
3. For islands, ask: closer to Southeast Asia, or out in the Pacific near Australia?
4. Himalayan states (Nepal, Bhutan) sit between India and China, so they are Asian.

Watch out: Papua New Guinea shares an island with Indonesia but counts as Oceania.

## The Americas
1. The split is at the Panama–Colombia border: everything north of it, including Central America and the Caribbean, is **North America**.
2. So Mexico, Guatemala, Belize, Cuba and Panama are North American.
3. Colombia and everything south of it is **South America** (Peru, Paraguay, Uruguay, Argentina).
4. Suriname and Guyana sit on South America's north coast, so they are South American.

Watch out: "Central America" is not a separate continent in this quiz; answer North America.

## Oceans and seas
1. Size order, biggest first: Pacific, Atlantic, Indian, Southern, Arctic.
2. Place them: Pacific between Asia/Australia and the Americas; Atlantic between Europe/Africa and the Americas; Indian south of Asia, between Africa and Australia; Arctic around the North Pole.
3. A sea or bay belongs to the ocean it opens into: the Caribbean opens to the Atlantic, the Bay of Bengal to the Indian Ocean.
4. Named seas: the Mediterranean lies between Europe and Africa; the Red Sea between Arabia and northeast Africa.

Watch out: a strait is a narrow channel linking two seas, like the one between Spain and Morocco.
