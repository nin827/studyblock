# Word Roots

Many English words are built from Latin and Greek parts. Learn a root once and you can often work out the meaning of words you have never seen before: *spect* (look) gives you inspect, spectator and spectacles.

| Level | What to expect |
| --- | --- |
| Easy | Very common roots and affixes |
| Medium | Roots in everyday academic words |
| Hard | Less familiar roots, and finding the word that contains a root |

> [!TIP]
> A prefix comes at the start of a word (*un*happy), a suffix at the end (care*less*).
