# Recipes and Scaling

Kitchen maths: making a recipe for more or fewer people, swapping between cups, spoons and grams, converting oven temperatures, and planning when to start cooking.

| Idea | How |
| --- | --- |
| Scale factor | new servings ÷ recipe servings |
| Scaled amount | amount × scale factor |
| Ratio a : b | total ÷ (a + b) gives one part |
| °C to °F | °F = °C × 9 / 5 + 32 |
| °F to °C | °C = (°F − 32) × 5 / 9 |

Every question states the kitchen factors it uses, for example **1 cup = 16 tbsp**, **1 tbsp = 3 tsp**, **1 cup = 240 mL** or **1 cup of flour = 125 g**. Cup and spoon sizes differ between countries, so always use the factor given.

**Rounding:** each question says how to round (nearest gram, 1 or 2 decimal places, nearest 5 °C). Eggs, bags and trays are whole, so read whether to round up. Times are typed in 24-hour format, for example `17:45`.

| Level | What to expect |
| --- | --- |
| Easy | Doubling and halving, whole spoons and cups, simple bake times |
| Medium | Any number of servings, ratios, fractions of a cup, oven temperatures, roasting rules |
| Hard | The ingredient that runs out first, grams to cups, start times with resting |
| Expert | Party shopping, baker's percentages, convert then scale, a full dinner timetable |
