# Unit Conversion: how to solve

## General

- Find the conversion factor. Metric facts are fixed, and non-metric factors are given in the question.
- Going to a smaller unit gives a bigger number, so multiply. Going to a bigger unit gives a smaller number, so divide.
- For long conversions, go one step at a time through a unit you know (for example ft → in → cm → m).
- Easy and medium answers are exact, so type the full value. On hard questions round to 2 decimal places at the end.

## Length

1. Find the factor: 1 cm = 10 mm, 1 m = 100 cm, 1 km = 1000 m, or the inch, foot or mile factor in the question.
2. Decide the direction: to a smaller unit, multiply. To a bigger unit, divide.
3. For two steps (cm → km), go via metres: ÷ 100, then ÷ 1000.

Example: 3.5 km to m is `3.5 × 1000 = 3500` m.

**Watch out:** check the size of your answer. 2500 mm should become a small number of metres (2.5), not 2 500 000.

## Mass and volume

1. Use 1 kg = 1000 g, 1 tonne = 1000 kg, 1 L = 1000 mL and 1 m³ = 1000 L.
2. 1 cm³ is exactly 1 mL, so that step leaves the number unchanged.
3. For mixed amounts, convert every part to the target unit first, then add.

Example: 2 kg 350 g in grams is `2 × 1000 + 350 = 2350` g.

**Watch out:** don't add numbers in different units (2 kg + 350 g is not 352).

## Time and speed

1. Use 1 min = 60 s and 1 h = 60 min = 3600 s. Time is base 60, not base 10.
2. km/h to m/s: divide by 3.6. m/s to km/h: multiply by 3.6.
3. For a speed from a distance and a time, put the time in the right unit first (hours for km/h), then divide distance by time.

Example: 90 km/h is `90 / 3.6 = 25` m/s.

**Watch out:** 1.5 h is 90 minutes, not 150. And 2 h 35 min is `2 × 60 + 35` minutes.

## Temperature

1. °C to °F: `°F = °C × 9/5 + 32`.
2. °F to °C: `°C = (°F − 32) × 5/9`. Subtract 32 first.
3. °C to K: add 273.15. K to °C: subtract 273.15.
4. °F and K: go via °C in two steps.

Example: 25 °C is `25 × 9 / 5 + 32 = 77` °F.

**Watch out:** the order matters. For °F to °C, take away 32 before multiplying by 5/9.
