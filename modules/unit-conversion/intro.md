# Unit Conversion

Convert quantities between units. Going to a smaller unit gives a bigger number (multiply); going to a bigger unit gives a smaller number (divide). Every question that uses a non-metric unit states its conversion factor.

| Quantity | Facts |
| --- | --- |
| Length | 1 cm = 10 mm, 1 m = 100 cm, 1 km = 1000 m |
| Imperial length | 1 in = 2.54 cm, 1 ft = 12 in, 1 mile = 1.609 km (given in the question) |
| Mass | 1 kg = 1000 g, 1 tonne = 1000 kg |
| Volume | 1 L = 1000 mL, 1 cm³ = 1 mL, 1 m³ = 1000 L |
| Time | 1 min = 60 s, 1 h = 60 min = 3600 s |
| Speed | 1 m/s = 3.6 km/h, so km/h ÷ 3.6 = m/s |
| Temperature | °F = °C × 9/5 + 32, °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9, K = °C + 273.15 |

| Level | What to expect |
| --- | --- |
| Easy | One step between neighbouring units; temperature formulas are given |
| Medium | Two-step metric conversions, inches and feet, km/h and m/s, mixed units like 2 h 35 min |
| Hard | Miles, feet to metres, sums of mixed units, speeds from distance and time; answers rounded to 2 decimal places |

> [!TIP]
> Easy and medium answers come out exactly, so type the full value. On hard questions, round to 2 decimal places (answers within 0.005 are accepted).
