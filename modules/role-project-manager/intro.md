# Applied scenarios (Project Manager)

Expert+ questions for the **Project Manager** role. Each one is a single scenario with several linked answers, and each answer uses a different skill: reading an activity network, counting working days on a calendar, crashing for the lowest cost, earned value, or turning effort hours into team days. A later part often builds on an earlier one, so work in order. The calculator is on, and the numbers from the prompt are already in it where that helps.

All figures are made up for practice. Every rule is stated in the question: work runs Monday to Friday with the start date as day 1, holidays are listed, a task takes whole working days (rounded up), and every escalation, penalty or bonus rule is spelled out. Money is in a generic `$`. This is general textbook project management, not any one tool's behaviour or any country's rules.

## Delays and finish dates

A network gives the duration, a critical path or a float. A slip then moves the finish date in working days. Expert+ adds a contract deadline: the latest start, the days late, and whether to pay the penalty or crash the activity.

## Crashing and cost

Crash options with a cost per day give the cheapest way to cut one or more days. Later parts add the total cost, the overheads saved, a crashing budget (working backwards to the days it buys) and a bonus decision.

## Earned value and forecasts

Share of work done and actual cost give EV, CPI and the forecast at completion, then a status. Expert+ starts from a stated forecast and works back to the CPI and the actual cost, then applies an escalation rule.

## Team effort and calendar

Effort hours and team size give working days (rounded up) and finish dates, then labour cost. Expert+ works back from a deadline to the working days available and the smallest team.
