# Elements: how to solve

## General
- A symbol is one capital letter, or a capital plus a lower-case letter (`Co` is cobalt, `CO` is carbon monoxide).
- Most symbols come from the English name; the odd ones come from **Latin** (or German) names.
- Typed answers ignore capital letters, so `fe` and `Fe` both count, but spelling of names still counts.

## Symbols
1. Try the first letter of the name (C carbon, O oxygen, N nitrogen, H hydrogen).
2. If that letter is taken, add a later letter from the name (Ca calcium, Cl chlorine, Mg magnesium).
3. If neither fits, think Latin (or German): the symbol comes from the old name.

| Element | Symbol | From |
| --- | --- | --- |
| sodium | Na | *natrium* |
| potassium | K | *kalium* |
| iron | Fe | *ferrum* (ferrous) |
| copper | Cu | *cuprum* |
| silver | Ag | *argentum* (Argentina) |
| gold | Au | *aurum* |
| tin | Sn | *stannum* |
| lead | Pb | *plumbum* (plumber) |
| mercury | Hg | *hydrargyrum* |
| antimony | Sb | *stibium* |
| tungsten | W | *wolfram* (German) |

Watch out: S is sulfur, Sn is tin, Sb is antimony, Na is sodium.

## Atomic numbers
1. The atomic number is the number of **protons**, and the periodic table is in that order.
2. Learn the first 20 as a chant: H He Li Be B C N O F Ne Na Mg Al Si P S Cl Ar K Ca.
3. Count along from a landmark you know: carbon 6, oxygen 8, neon 10, sodium 11, argon 18, calcium 20.
4. Useful later landmarks: iron 26, copper 29, silver 47, gold 79, mercury 80, lead 82, uranium 92.
5. Noble gases sit at 2, 10, 18, 36, 54, 86, which helps place nearby elements.

Watch out: the atomic number is not the mass number; carbon is 6, not 12.

## Groups and families
1. Find the column. Elements in the same column behave alike (main families in the table below).
2. Clues: very reactive soft metal → group 1; unreactive gas → group 18; "salt-forming" non-metal → halogen.
3. Hydrogen sits above group 1 but is not an alkali metal.

| Group | Family | Members |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | alkali metals | Li, Na, K, Rb, Cs |
| 2 | alkaline earth metals | Be, Mg, Ca, Sr, Ba |
| 3–12 | transition metals | Fe, Co, Ni, Cu, Zn, Ag, Au |
| 17 | halogens | F, Cl, Br, I |
| 18 | noble gases | He, Ne, Ar, Kr, Xe, Rn |

Watch out: "alkali" (group 1) and "alkaline earth" (group 2) are different families.
