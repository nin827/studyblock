# Accountant scenarios

Questions for the Accountant role. Each one is a single case with two to four linked parts, and each part uses a different skill: double entry, ratios, depreciation, break-even or audit judgement. Later parts often build on earlier ones, so work them in order.

Every figure is made up. Every rule you need (a lender's covenant, a materiality limit, a depreciation method) is stated in the question, and no country-specific law or tax is used. As elsewhere in StudyBlock, goods bought go to Inventory, a sale records revenue only (ignore the cost of the goods), and depreciation builds up in Accumulated depreciation.

## Transaction and covenant check

Record a transaction as a **debit and a credit**, see how it moves current assets and current liabilities, and test the current ratio against a lender's covenant. Expert+ works backwards to the largest purchase the covenant allows.

## Audit red flags

Work out **receivable days** and the **current ratio**, compare them with the stated warning limits, and choose the audit response. Expert+ corrects a sale recorded too early and judges whether the error is material.

## Fixed asset review

Charge **straight-line or reducing-balance depreciation**, find the carrying value and its effect on profit. Expert+ finds the life from the yearly charge and treats the gain or loss on disposal.

## Break-even and budget

Find **break-even units**, the margin of safety and the units needed for a target profit, then decide. Expert+ works backwards from a budgeted profit to the fixed costs and judges a special order.
