# Accountant scenarios: how to solve

## General

- Read the whole case first, then answer the parts in order: later parts use earlier results.
- The calculator starts with the scenario's numbers as rows (`_ca`, `_price`, …). Build each step on those labels and round only when you type an answer.
- Every rule you need is in the question. Apply it exactly: "at least" includes the limit, and "below" doesn't.
- Conventions: goods bought go to Inventory, a sale records revenue only, depreciation builds up in Accumulated depreciation.

## Transaction and covenant check

1. **Journal:** a debit increases an asset or an expense and decreases a liability, capital or revenue. A credit does the opposite. Pick the debit account and the credit account.
2. **Current assets** are cash, inventory and trade receivables. **Current liabilities** are trade payables and loans due within 12 months. Equipment and capital are neither.
3. **Current ratio** = current assets ÷ current liabilities. Working capital = current assets − current liabilities.
4. **Covenant:** compare the new ratio with the limit. "At least" means equal passes.
5. **Working backwards:** a credit purchase of X adds X to both sides, so solve (CA + X) ÷ (CL + X) = limit for X.

## Audit red flags

1. **Receivable days** = trade receivables ÷ revenue × 365.
2. **Current ratio** = current assets ÷ current liabilities.
3. Compare each figure with the stated limit. A rise of "more than" N days must exceed N.
4. **Response:** a receivables flag calls for confirmations and a cut-off test. A liquidity flag calls for a going-concern review of the cash-flow forecast. Both flags call for both.
5. **Early sale:** reverse it with a debit to Sales revenue and a credit to Trade receivables. Revenue, profit and receivables all fall by the amount. Material means the profit effect is above the stated percentage of reported profit.

## Fixed asset review

1. **Straight-line:** (cost − residual value) ÷ life, the same every year.
2. **Carrying value** = cost − accumulated depreciation.
3. **Reducing balance:** each year's charge = rate × the carrying value at the start of that year. The residual value is ignored.
4. **Profit** after depreciation = profit before depreciation − the year's charge.
5. **Disposal:** gain or loss = proceeds − carrying value. A gain adds to profit and a loss reduces it. Working backwards: life = (cost − residual) ÷ yearly charge.

## Break-even and budget

1. **Contribution per unit** = price − variable cost per unit.
2. **Break-even units** = fixed costs ÷ contribution per unit.
3. **Margin of safety** = budgeted units − break-even units, as a % of budgeted units.
4. **Target profit units** = (fixed costs + target profit) ÷ contribution per unit.
5. **Working backwards:** budgeted profit = budgeted units × contribution − fixed costs, so fixed costs = units × contribution − profit.
6. **Special order:** with spare capacity and no new fixed costs, only the extra contribution (price − variable cost) less any one-off cost matters. Ignore the average full cost.
