## General

Travel scenarios chain simple steps, so work in order and keep the units straight (minutes, local times, $, percentages, days).

1. Read the stated rules first: UTC offsets, minimum connection times, child percentages, fees, bands and due dates.
2. Write down which number each part asks for, and its rounding.
3. For clock times, work in minutes and convert back to `HH:MM` at the end (60 minutes to an hour, 24 hours to a day).
4. For a yes or no or a pick-one, find both numbers first, then compare them using the stated rule.
5. Round only where the part says to; money in dollars and cents is exact here.

## Itineraries and time zones

Local arrival = local departure + flight time + (arrival offset − departure offset). A time past 24:00 is the next day; a time below 00:00 is the day before. Offsets are fixed, with no daylight saving.

The layover is the gap between landing and the next departure, both in local time at that one airport, so no time shift is needed for it. A connection is allowed only if the layover is at least the minimum connection time.

Total travel time is elapsed time: both (or all) flight times plus the layovers. The time zones cancel out, so ignore the offsets. For a later flight, start from its own departure time and add its flight time and the offset difference between its two places.

## Package prices

A child's fare is the stated percentage of the adult price. Flights = adults × fare + children × child fare. Transfers and single supplements apply only to the people stated.

Rooms are whole rooms: people ÷ room size, rounded up. Room cost = rooms × rate × nights. A promotion takes its percentage off the whole bundle price.

Per person = total ÷ everyone travelling (children too), rounded up as asked. Saving % = (dearer − cheaper) ÷ dearer × 100.

## Currency and fees

Units received = dollars exchanged × rate, after any fee. A percentage fee is that % of the dollars handed over; a flat fee is a fixed number of dollars. Both come off the dollars before converting.

Buying back: dollars = units ÷ units per dollar at the buy-back rate, rounded to the cent. Net cost = dollars handed over − dollars received back. A round-trip loss is the dollars handed over minus the dollars received at the end; loss % = loss ÷ dollars handed over × 100.

## Commission and booking dates

Commission = its percentage of the stated base; a deposit is a percentage of the total, and the balance is the total minus the deposit.

Count back from the departure date for a due date, day by day through the months (check each month's length, and leap years). Days before departure = the gap from the cancellation date to the departure date.

A cancellation charge is the percentage of the price for the band the number of days falls in; check the band edges ("30 or more" includes 30). Refund = amount paid − charge. Commission on a group booking after a cancellation is paid on what the supplier keeps: the remaining travellers' prices plus the charge.
