# Applied scenarios (Travel Agent)

Scenario questions for the **Travel Agent** role, from Medium to Expert+. Each one is a single made-up trip or booking with several linked answers, and each answer uses a different skill: shifting clock times between time zones, working out percentages of a fare, converting money at a rate, counting calendar days, and a yes or no or pick-one decision. A later part usually builds on an earlier one, so work in order. The calculator is on, and the numbers from the prompt are already in it where that helps.

Money is in a generic `$`, times are in 24-hour `HH:MM`, and every rule is stated in the question, with each part saying how to round.

## Itineraries and time zones

A flight leaves at a local time and lands in another time zone, so you add the flight time and then the difference between the two fixed UTC offsets. Later there is a layover, a minimum connection time that decides whether a connection is allowed, and the total travel time. At Expert+ there are three flights, two airports with their own minimums, and half-hour offsets.

## Package prices

Adults pay a full fare and children a stated percentage. You price a family trip, compare building it yourself with a bundle, and compare two operators that add single supplements or a booking fee. At Expert+ you also count whole hotel rooms, add transfers, apply a promotion and find the saving as a percentage.

## Currency and fees

Dollars are exchanged for a made-up currency at a stated rate, after a commission or flat fee. You compare a desk with a card withdrawal, buy leftover money back at a worse rate, and at Expert+ compare the round-trip loss at two desks.

## Commission and booking dates

The agency earns commission on a booking, and the traveller pays a deposit and a balance. You count back from the departure date to a balance due date, count the days between a cancellation and departure, find the cancellation charge from a table of bands, and at Expert+ work out commission on what the supplier keeps when one traveller cancels.
