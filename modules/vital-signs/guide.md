# Vital Signs: how to solve

## General

- Find the formula in the question and match each number to its place in it.
- Watch the units: mL ÷ 1000 gives litres, ms ÷ 1000 gives seconds, and hours × 60 gives minutes.
- Keep full precision and round only at the end, as the question asks.
- These are calculations only; they say nothing about whether a reading is healthy.

## Heart rate

1. For a count, multiply the beats by 60 and divide by the seconds counted.
2. For the time between beats, divide 60 by the beats per minute.
3. For a rate from the time between beats, divide 60 by that time in seconds (change ms to s first).

Example: 18 beats in 15 seconds gives 18 × 60 ÷ 15 = 72 beats per minute.

**Watch out:** divide by the number of seconds counted, not by 60.

## Circulation

1. Cardiac output = heart rate × stroke volume ÷ 1000, in L/min.
2. Rearrange it for stroke volume (cardiac output × 1000 ÷ heart rate) or heart rate (cardiac output × 1000 ÷ stroke volume).
3. Pulse pressure = systolic − diastolic.
4. MAP ≈ diastolic + (systolic − diastolic) ÷ 3.

Example: 120/80 gives MAP ≈ 80 + 40 ÷ 3 ≈ 93.3 mmHg.

**Watch out:** MAP is not the plain average of the two numbers. Divide the difference by 3 and add it to the diastolic value.

## Breathing

1. For a count, multiply the breaths by 60 and divide by the seconds counted.
2. Minute ventilation = breathing rate × tidal volume ÷ 1000, in L/min.
3. Rearrange it for tidal volume or breathing rate when one of those is missing.
4. Alveolar ventilation = rate × (tidal volume − dead space) ÷ 1000.

Example: 12 breaths a minute at 500 mL gives 12 × 500 ÷ 1000 = 6 L/min.

**Watch out:** take the dead space away from each breath before multiplying by the rate.

## Temperature

1. °C to °F: multiply by 9/5, then add 32.
2. °F to °C: subtract 32, then multiply by 5/9.
3. A change in temperature: multiply by 9/5 or 5/9 only, with no 32.
4. A change per hour: (later − earlier) ÷ hours.

Example: 37 °C × 9/5 + 32 = 98.6 °F.

**Watch out:** a rise of 1 °C is a rise of 1.8 °F, not 33.8 °F.
