# Logic Puzzles

Every puzzle here has exactly **one** answer, and it can be found by careful reasoning alone: no guessing, no outside knowledge. Each one was checked by trying every possible answer.

## Knights and knaves

On this island, **knights** always tell the truth and **knaves** always lie. Work out who is who from what they say.

| Level | Islanders | Statements |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Easy | 2 | about one person, "the same kind", counts |
| Medium | 3 | adds "or" |
| Hard | 4 | adds "if … then"; every statement is needed |
| Expert | 5 | adds "B would say that …"; you list the knights from blocks |

## Ordering puzzles

People finish a race or stand in a queue. Use the clues to put them in order, building the order from name blocks (some questions ask who is in one place instead). Every clue is needed.

| Level | People | Clues |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Easy | 3 | first, last, directly in front, somewhere ahead |
| Medium | 4 | adds exact places and "not first/last" |
| Hard | 5 | adds "next to", "not next to" and "exactly k between" |
| Expert | 6 | no clue gives a place: only how people relate to each other |

## Whodunits

One suspect did it, each suspect makes one statement, and you're told how many are lying. Try each suspect as the culprit.

**Hard** and **Expert** include statements about other statements ("Ben is lying"). At **Expert** you also find the second liar.
