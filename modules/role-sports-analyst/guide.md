## General

Sports scenarios chain simple steps, so work in order and keep the units straight (seconds, minutes, kilometres, points, per 90, percent).

1. Read the stated rules first: the points for a win and a draw, the tie-break, the rating formula, the threshold or margin.
2. Convert before you calculate: 1 minute = 60 seconds, 1 hour = 3600 seconds, percent ÷ 100 = a decimal chance.
3. Round only as each part asks, and use the unrounded values for later steps unless told otherwise.
4. For a verdict, find the number first, then compare it with the stated rule.

## Race pace and splits

Pace = time ÷ distance, usually in seconds per km, then written as minutes and seconds. Time = pace × distance. Work in seconds, and change back with minutes = seconds ÷ 60 (whole part) and the remainder.

Two halves at different paces: add the two half times for the finish, then divide by the full distance for the average pace. A smaller time per km is faster, and a negative split means a faster second half.

Working backwards from a target: take away the stopped time, then the time already used, and divide what is left by the distance left. A pace is possible if the runner can run at least that fast.

## League table and run-in

Points = 3 × wins + 1 × draws. Goal difference = goals scored − goals conceded (negative if more are conceded). For results, count each team's own games: add the goals for and against, and the points from each result. Rank by points, then goal difference, then goals scored, only using the next rule when the one before is level.

For a run-in, the most points a team can reach = its points + 3 × its games left. The leader needs at least the rival's best total (a tie goes to the leader if stated). Wins needed = points needed ÷ 3, rounded up, when the other games are lost.

## Player statistics and ratings

Rate per 90 = count × 90 ÷ minutes played. Shooting accuracy = goals ÷ shots × 100. Compare players on the rate, not the total: more minutes means more goals even at a lower rate.

For a stated rating formula, weight each count, add, then scale to 90 minutes, using the raw counts. Percentage difference = (new − current) ÷ current × 100, and it is negative when the new value is lower.

## Expected points and chances

Expected points for one match = 3 × (win chance) + 1 × (draw chance), with chances as decimals. Over several matches the expected points add.

For independent matches, the chance of several things all happening is the product. The chance of at least one win = 1 − (the chance of no wins). To reach a total of points, list every result pair that gives it, multiply within each pair, and add the pairs.
