# Applied scenarios (Sports Analyst)

Scenario questions for the **Sports Analyst** role, from Medium to Expert+. Each one is a single made-up sporting scenario with several linked answers, and each answer uses a different skill: converting times, rates and percentages, tallying a table, comparing players and multiplying chances. A later part usually builds on an earlier one, so work in order. The calculator is on, and the numbers from the prompt are already in it where that helps.

All teams and players are invented and every figure is made up. The scoring rules, rating formula, thresholds and match chances are stated in each question as rules for the exercise. The chances are used to find expected points and the likelihood of results, nothing more.

## Race pace and splits

Pace is time per kilometre, and time is pace times distance, so most of the work is converting between minutes and seconds. Races are run in two halves at different paces (a negative split is a faster second half), compared with a target time, and at Expert+ planned backwards from a target with the time lost at aid stations.

## League table and run-in

Three points for a win, one for a draw and none for a loss, with a stated tie-break. You tally a record, then a whole table from a list of results, then rank the teams. At Expert+ a team has games left: the most points a rival can still reach, the points the leader needs to stay ahead, and whether the leader's expected return is enough.

## Player statistics and ratings

Totals hide how often a player does something, so rates are scaled to 90 minutes and shooting accuracy compares goals with shots. You compare two players on a fair scale, then use a stated rating formula, and at Expert+ work out the percentage difference between a current player and a transfer target against a signing rule.

## Expected points and chances

An expected value weights each result's points by its stated chance. Over several independent matches, expected points add and chances multiply. You find the chance of winning every game, of at least one win or of staying unbeaten, and at Expert+ add up the different ways of reaching a qualifying total of points.
