# Applied scenarios (Electrician)

Scenario questions for the **Electrician** role, from Medium to Expert+. Each one is a single made-up scenario with several linked answers, and each answer uses a different skill: adding appliance loads to a current, choosing a breaker or cable from a stated list or table, percentages of a supply voltage, series and parallel resistance with Ohm's law, and energy cost and payback. A later part usually builds on an earlier one, so work in order. The calculator is on, and the numbers from the prompt are already in it where that helps.

The supply is 230 V and money is in a generic `$`. Breaker ratings, the cable table (current rating and mV/A/m), the 80% load rule and voltage-drop limits are all stated in each question, and each part says how to round. Nothing here is advice for real installations.

## Circuit load and breaker

Appliance powers give a total current at 230 V, then the smallest breaker from a stated list. At Expert you apply an 80% load rule and recheck after an extra appliance. At Expert+ you start from the breaker, find the spare power, how many heaters fit, and judge a plan against both the breaker rule and a cable rating.

## Cable and voltage drop

A load current and run length give the smallest cable from a stated table, then the voltage drop, its percentage of the supply and a check against a limit. At Expert+ you work backwards from the limit to the largest mV/A/m figure, pick the cable that passes both tests and judge another cable.

## Resistor networks and faults

Series and parallel resistors give a total resistance, the supply current, the p.d. across a pair and a branch current. At Expert+ a measured current points to one of four faults, and you check which one fits.

## Running cost and payback

Power, hours and a price per kWh give energy and cost. Motor efficiency gives the input power, and an old and a new motor give a yearly saving and a payback time. At Expert+ you work backwards from a stated saving to the running hours, then judge the payback against a limit.
