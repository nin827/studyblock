# Applied scenarios (Economist)

Expert+ questions for the **Economist** role. Each one is a single scenario with several linked answers, and each answer uses a different skill: index numbers, percentages, the midpoint method, linear supply and demand, compound growth or a judgement call. A later part often builds on an earlier one, so work in order. The calculator is on, and the numbers from the prompt are already in it where that helps.

All figures are made up for practice, and every rule (real wage = wage ÷ index × 100, the midpoint method, the rule of 70, the pricing rule) is stated in the question. Every part says how to round. Money is in a generic `$`.

## Inflation and real wages

A price index gives inflation, and dividing a wage by the index gives its real value. You decide whether workers are better or worse off. At Expert+ you work backwards from a stated nominal and real wage change to the price index.

## Elasticity and revenue

Two price and quantity points give a midpoint elasticity, then the change in revenue, then a pricing call. At Expert+ the elasticity is given and you find the new quantity, then judge what to do next.

## Taxes and who pays

Linear demand and supply curves give the market after a per-unit tax: the price buyers pay, the quantity, the tax revenue and the share the buyers bear. At Expert+ you work out who bears the tax from what was observed.

## Growth and doubling time

An economy compounds at a steady rate. Find its size after some years, its GDP per person, its growth in per-person terms and how long it takes to double. At Expert+ you recover the growth rate from two figures and judge living standards.
