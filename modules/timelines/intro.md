# Timelines

Reason with dates like a historian: gaps between events, centuries and decades, and the order things happened in. The events are well-known ones with undisputed years, from the Battle of Marathon (490 BC) to the opening of the Channel Tunnel (1994).

**The dates are given to you** in every topic except **Famous dates (recall)**, so the skill is the date reasoning, not memory. Famous dates is the one topic that tests what you remember.

**The BC/AD rule:** there is **no year 0**. The year after 1 BC is AD 1. So from 44 BC to AD 79 is 44 + 79 − 1 = 122 years, not 123.

| Idea | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Century | The 1st century is years 1–100, so 1450 is in the 15th century and 1900 in the 19th. |
| BC century | The 1st century BC is 100–1 BC, so 490 BC is in the 5th century BC. |
| Decade | The 1960s run from 1960 to 1969. |

**Answer formats**

- Year gaps, ages and years are whole numbers.
- Centuries and millennia: type just the number (`15` for the 15th century).
- Decades: type the first year (`1960` for the 1960s).
- Orders are picked from a list of letters, like `C, A, D, B`.
