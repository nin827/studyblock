# Applied scenarios (Land Surveyor)

Expert+ questions for the **Land Surveyor** role. Each one is a single scenario with several linked answers, and each answer uses a different skill: bearings and trigonometry to get coordinates, column sums and Pythagoras for a traverse, the coordinate method for areas, or a level book with a check at the end. A later part usually builds on an earlier one, so work in order. The calculator is on, and the numbers from the prompt are already in it where that helps.

This role practises the arithmetic only: every figure is made up and every rule is stated in the question, so none of it is a survey standard for a real job. Every part says how to round. Coordinates and distances are in metres on a local grid, bearings are whole-circle bearings measured clockwise from grid north, and money is in a generic `$`. Accuracy limits, allowances and plot rules are stated in each question; real limits depend on the country and the type of survey.

## Bearings and coordinates

A bearing and a distance give the change in Easting and Northing, and so a new point. At higher levels you carry on to the distance and bearing to a control point, or work backwards from two sets of coordinates to a bearing and distance and set out an offset peg.

## Traverse closure

The components of the legs of a closed traverse should add up to zero. The sums give the closing error, Pythagoras gives the misclosure, and perimeter ÷ misclosure gives the linear accuracy 1 in N. At Expert+ you work back to the largest misclosure a stated accuracy allows and judge the traverse.

## Plot area by coordinates

The shoelace (coordinate) method gives the area of a plot from its corners, then hectares, a land value, or the number of whole plots after roads. At Expert+ one Northing is missing and you find it from the stated area.

## Level books and misclosure

Staff readings are turned into reduced levels, by height of instrument or by rise and fall, with the arithmetic check (ΣBS − ΣFS equals the last RL minus the first). At Expert+ you compare the misclosure with a stated allowance of 10 mm × √K and accept or reject the run.
