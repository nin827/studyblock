# Applied scenarios (Astronomer)

Scenario questions for the **Astronomer** role, from Medium to Expert+. Each one is a single scenario with several linked answers, and each answer uses a different skill: inverses, logarithms, powers of ten, ratios and squares, rounding up to whole nights, and a yes or no or pick-one decision. A later part often builds on an earlier one, so work in order. The calculator is on, and the numbers from the prompt are already in it where that helps.

Every part says how to round. If you reuse a rounded earlier answer, the later answer still comes out the same. Very large or small numbers are typed as A in A × 10ⁿ, with n given.

## Parallax and star distances

A star's parallax gives its distance in parsecs, then light-years and the year its light set out. At Expert you compare two stars against a survey range. At Expert+ you work back from a light-travel time to a parallax, and on to a second star.

## Magnitudes and brightness

Which star looks brighter, and by what factor, from a magnitude difference. Then the distance modulus, absolute and apparent magnitudes. At Expert+ you work back from m and M to the distance, the fading factor and a survey range.

## Telescope optics

Magnification from two focal lengths, light-gathering power against the eye, and the smallest separation a telescope can split. At Expert you pick an eyepiece from a case. At Expert+ you compare two telescopes.

## Observing schedules

Stars rise 4 minutes earlier each night, so a schedule is counted in nights and written as 24-hour HH:MM times. Round nights up. At Expert+ you recover the number of nights from two sightings and cross midnight.
