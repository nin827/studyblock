# Vocabulary

Build a wider and more precise English vocabulary. Pick the right definition, spot synonyms and antonyms, untangle words that are easy to mix up, and choose the word that fits a sentence.

| Level | What to expect |
| --- | --- |
| Easy | Everyday words |
| Medium | Words common in books, news and school writing |
| Hard | Less common, more formal words |

**Tip:** in sentence questions, the blank is shown as ____.
