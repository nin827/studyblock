# Electricity

Practise the core formulas of electric circuits. Each question gives you some quantities and asks for one missing value. Voltage is in volts (V), current in amps (A), resistance in ohms (Ω), power in watts (W) and energy in kilowatt-hours (kWh).

| Formula | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `V = I × R` | Ohm's law: voltage = current × resistance |
| `P = V × I` | power = voltage × current |
| `P = I² × R` | power from current and resistance |
| `P = V² / R` | power from voltage and resistance |
| `E = P × t` | energy (kWh) = power (kW) × time (h) |
| `R = R1 + R2 + …` | resistors in series |
| `R = (R1 × R2) / (R1 + R2)` | two resistors in parallel |

Useful conversions: 1 kΩ = 1000 Ω, 1 mA = 0.001 A, 1 kW = 1000 W. Energy cost = energy in kWh × price per kWh.

| Level | What to expect |
| --- | --- |
| Easy | Small whole numbers, series circuits, whole-number answers |
| Medium | mA and kΩ, P = V²/R, two resistors in parallel, still whole-number answers |
| Hard | Decimal values, energy and cost in kWh, combined circuits, answers rounded to 2 decimal places |

**Tip:** on hard questions, round to 2 decimal places (answers within 0.005 are accepted). Two resistors in parallel always have less resistance than either one alone.
