# Kinematics

Practise the formulas that describe how things move. Each question gives you some quantities and asks for one missing value. Distances are in metres (m), times in seconds (s), speeds in m/s and accelerations in m/s².

| Formula | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `v = d / t` | speed = distance ÷ time |
| `a = (v - u) / t` | acceleration = change in velocity ÷ time |
| `v = u + at` | final velocity after accelerating for t seconds |
| `s = ut + ½at²` | distance covered while accelerating uniformly |
| `v² = u² + 2as` | links velocities and distance without needing time |

Here u is the initial velocity, v the final velocity, a the acceleration, t the time and s (or d) the distance.

| Level | What to expect |
| --- | --- |
| Easy | Small whole numbers, whole-number answers |
| Medium | Bigger numbers and deceleration, still whole-number answers |
| Hard | Decimal values, answers rounded to 2 decimal places |

**Tip:** on hard questions, round to 2 decimal places (answers within 0.005 are accepted). A slowing-down object has a negative acceleration.
