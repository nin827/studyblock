# Grammar

Practice the building blocks of clear English sentences: what job each word does, which verb tense to use, how to punctuate, and how to make subjects, verbs and pronouns agree.

| Level | What to expect |
| --- | --- |
| Easy | Everyday sentences and the most common rules |
| Medium | Perfect and continuous tenses, semicolons, tricky subjects |
| Hard | Words that change their part of speech, and agreement traps |

**Tip:** in parts-of-speech questions, the word to identify is shown in **bold**. The examples follow standard US English.
