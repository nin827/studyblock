# Applied scenarios (Civil Engineer)

Expert+ questions for the **Civil Engineer** role. Each one is a single scenario with several linked answers, and each answer uses a different skill: support reactions and bending moments, stress and strain, concrete volumes and truckloads, gradients and levels, trigonometry for setting out, pipe flows and pumps, or ground stresses and slopes. A later part usually builds on an earlier one, so work in order. The calculator is on, and the numbers from the prompt are already in it where that helps.

This role practises the arithmetic only: every figure is made up and every rule is stated in the question, so none of it is a design rule for a real job. Every part says how to round. Loads are static, lengths are in metres unless stated, loads in kN, stresses in N/mm² (MPa), and money in a generic `$`. Allowable stresses, yield stresses, E = 200 GPa, bulking, waste allowances and tolerances are stated in each question. Water questions use a density of 1000 kg/m³ and g = 9.81 N/kg; ground questions use unit weights in kN/m³, γw = 9.81 kN/m³ and stresses in kPa (1 kPa = 1 kN/m²).

## Beams and section checks

A simply supported beam with a point load and a uniform load gives the reactions, then the bending moment, then the section modulus needed for a stated allowable stress, and a verdict on a stated section. At Expert+ you work backwards from a section's capacity to the largest load, then judge bending and the supports.

## Tie rods and factor of safety

A steel tie rod gives stress, extension and a factor of safety against a stated minimum. At Expert+ you size the rod from the minimum factor of safety, pick a standard diameter and check its stretch against a limit.

## Concrete quantities and loads

A slab, and at Expert and above some pad footings, give a volume, an order with a waste allowance, the number of trucks (always rounded up) and the bill. At Expert+ you work backwards from trucks delivered and concrete left over.

## Levels, cut and fill, setting out

A gradient of 1 in N gives a level difference and a formation level, then cut or fill, then the volume of a cutting, its loosened volume and the lorry loads. At Expert+ you set out a peg: from two levels to the slope angle and the tape length, then a tolerance call.

## Pipes and drainage

A full pipe gives a flow rate and a filling time. A drain's invert levels give its gradient as 1 in N, the invert at the outfall and a flood-clearance check. A pump gives the total head, the hydraulic and electrical power and the daily running cost. At Expert+ you find the steady supply rate for a demand profile, size the main backwards from a velocity limit, work out the tank storage from a running total and judge an existing tank.

## Ground and slopes

Unit weights and a water table give the total stress, the pore water pressure and the effective stress at depth. A pad footing gives its own weight, the bearing pressure and a factor of safety against a stated minimum. At Expert+ a block on a slip plane gives its factor of safety, then you work backwards to the anchor force it needs, pick a stock ground anchor and judge a wider spacing.
