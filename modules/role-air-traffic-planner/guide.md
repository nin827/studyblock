## General

These are planning scenarios that chain simple steps, so work in order and keep the units straight (kt, nm, minutes, seconds, kg).

1. Read the stated rules first: the separation table, the slot capacity, the hold lap time, the reserve.
2. Convert before you calculate: time in hours = minutes ÷ 60; distance ÷ speed gives hours, × 60 for minutes, × 3600 for seconds.
3. Round only as each part asks (up for laps, down for capacities and speed caps). The chained answers here do not change if you use an earlier rounded answer.
4. For a verdict, find the number first, then compare it with the stated limit.

## Runway arrival spacing

Find the separation in the table by the leader's category first, then the follower's. At one common speed, time gap = separation ÷ speed (hours), × 3600 for seconds. For a stream, add the separations of each neighbouring pair, divide the total by the speed for the time span, and the average gap is the span ÷ the number of gaps. Arrivals per hour = 60 ÷ average gap in minutes, rounded down.

At Expert+ the follower is at a different speed. Flight 1 reaches the threshold after D ÷ its speed. In that time the follower flies D × (follower speed ÷ leader speed), and must still be the minimum separation behind, so the gate spacing = separation + that distance − D. The time gap at the gate = spacing ÷ the follower's speed, × 3600.

## Departure slots and delays

Take the flights in the listed order. Each takes the earliest slot at or after its request that still has room; closed slots hold nobody. Delay = slots pushed back × 15 minutes. Total delay is the sum, the longest is the largest, the average is total ÷ number of flights. A closure's cost is the total delay with it minus the total delay without it.

## In-trail separation on a route

Both aircraft fly the same way, so the gap changes at the difference of the speeds. Time until the minimum = (gap − minimum) ÷ closing speed, × 60 for minutes. In that time the follower gains at most gap − minimum, so the follower's top speed = leader's speed + (gap − minimum) × 60 ÷ minutes.

At Expert+ each aircraft's time to the fix = its distance to the fix ÷ its own speed; the follower is further away by the gap. The time gap at the fix is the difference, and the follower's top speed to keep the required gap = its distance ÷ (leader's time + required gap).

## Holding and diversion fuel

Laps = delay ÷ lap time, rounded up. Fuel per lap = burn rate × lap time ÷ 60. Fuel after the hold must cover the approach and the final reserve. Spare fuel = fuel on board − what must be kept; laps affordable = spare ÷ fuel per lap, rounded down.

At Expert+ the diversion fuel = distance ÷ speed × cruise burn rate, and it is kept back along with the final reserve before any laps are spent.
