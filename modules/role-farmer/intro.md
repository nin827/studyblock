# Applied scenarios (Farmer)

Scenario questions for the **Farmer** role, from Medium to Expert+. Each one is a single made-up scenario with several linked answers, and each answer uses a different skill: square metres to hectares, rates and percentages, rounding up to whole trips or bags, and yes or no decisions. A later part usually builds on an earlier one, so work in order. The calculator is on, and the numbers from the prompt are already in it where that helps.

Money is in a generic `$`. 1 ha = 10 000 m², 1 tonne = 1000 kg, and every rule is stated in the question, with each part saying how to round.

## Field area and yield

A field is measured in metres and turned into hectares, then multiplied by the yield to give the harvest. Trailer trips round up and the income follows from the price. Later a triangular headland is added, and at Expert+ two fields with different yields give an average yield and a contract check.

## Seed, fertiliser and spraying

A target rate for seed or fertiliser becomes a rate per hectare, a total for the field, a number of whole bags and a cost. At Expert+ a sprayer has a tank size, a working speed and refill stops, and you decide whether the job fits the spraying window.

## Livestock feed and grazing

A herd's daily ration becomes a total for the feeding period, then bales (rounded up) and their cost. At Expert+ a paddock's grass is only partly usable, so you count whole days of grazing, the feed to buy for the rest, and whether the stocking rate is within the limit.

## Crop gross margin

Output is yield times price, and gross margin is output minus variable costs. You scale it to the whole field, compare two crops and find which wins, and at Expert+ add fixed costs, a break-even yield and a price-fall check.
