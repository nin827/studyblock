## General

Farm scenarios chain simple steps, so work in order and keep the units straight (m², ha, kg, tonnes, litres, $).

1. Read the stated rules first: 1 ha = 10 000 m², the trailer, bag or tank size, the rates and limits.
2. Write down which number each part asks for, and its rounding.
3. Whole trips, bags, bales and tank loads round **up** when you need enough; whole days of grazing round **down**.
4. For a yes or no, find the numbers first, then compare using the stated rule.

## Field area and yield

Area of a rectangle = length × width in m², then ÷ 10 000 for hectares. A triangle is ½ × base × height. Harvest = hectares × yield per hectare. Trips = tonnes ÷ trailer capacity, rounded up. Income = tonnes × price per tonne.

With two fields, find each harvest at its own yield and add them. Average yield = total tonnes ÷ total hectares (not the average of the two yields). The contract is met if the total is at or above it.

## Seed, fertiliser and spraying

Fertiliser: product per hectare = nitrogen target ÷ the nitrogen fraction (percent ÷ 100). Seed: seeds per m² × 10 000 gives seeds per hectare; 1000 seeds weigh the stated grams, so kilograms per hectare = seeds per m² × (grams per 1000 seeds) ÷ 100. Total = rate × hectares. Bags = total ÷ bag size, rounded up. Cost = whole bags × price.

Sprayer: mix = hectares × litres per hectare; loads = mix ÷ tank size, rounded up. Time = hectares ÷ hectares per hour × 60 minutes, plus the refill stops (one fewer than the loads). Compare with the window in minutes.

## Livestock feed and grazing

Daily feed = animals × ration. Total = daily × days; ÷ 1000 for tonnes. Bales = total ÷ bale weight, rounded up; cost = whole bales × price.

Grazing: usable grass = hectares × cover per hectare × usable %. Days it lasts = usable ÷ daily intake, whole days only (round down). Feed to buy = daily intake × planned days − usable grass. Stocking rate = animals ÷ hectares; within the limit means at or below it.

## Crop gross margin

Output per hectare = yield × price. Gross margin = output − variable costs (add up every cost item). Whole field = margin per hectare × hectares.

For two crops, work out each margin per hectare and compare; the extra from the better one = the difference × hectares. Net profit = total gross margin − fixed costs. Break-even yield = (variable costs per hectare + fixed costs ÷ hectares) ÷ price. For a price fall, cut the price by the percentage and redo the profit.
