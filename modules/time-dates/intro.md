# Time and Dates

Everyday time sums: reading and converting clock times, working out how long things take, counting days on a real calendar, and changing time between cities.

| Fact | Value |
| --- | --- |
| 1 hour | 60 minutes |
| 1 day | 24 hours = 1440 minutes |
| 1 week | 7 days |
| 30-day months | April, June, September, November |
| February | 28 days, or 29 in a leap year |
| Leap year | divisible by 4, but century years only if divisible by 400 (2000 yes, 1900 no) |

**Conventions used in every question**

- 24-hour answers are typed `hh:mm`, for example `07:05` or `19:40` (`7:05`, `0705` and `07.05` are read too).
- 12-hour answers are typed with am or pm, for example `4:05 pm`. 12 am is just after midnight and 12 pm is just after midday.
- Dates are real calendar dates, leap years included. Type them as `5 January 2024`, `January 5, 2024` or `5/1/2024` (day first).
- "From the 1st to the 3rd" is 2 days. A question says when both the first and last day count.
- Time zones use the fixed offsets given in the question, with no daylight saving.

| Level | What to expect |
| --- | --- |
| Easy | Whole hours and nearby dates |
| Medium | Any minutes, 12 am and 12 pm, months of different lengths, days of the week |
| Hard | Past midnight, across years and leap years, flights between time zones |
| Expert | Working backwards, pay for shifts, ages, repeating events, working days and shared office hours |
