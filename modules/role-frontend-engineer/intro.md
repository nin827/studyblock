# Applied scenarios (Frontend Engineer)

Scenario questions for the **Frontend Engineer** role, from Medium to Expert+. Each one is a single made-up scenario with several linked answers, and each answer uses a different skill: percentages of a window, rem to pixels, rounding down to whole cards, units of data and time, ratios, and a yes or no decision. A later part usually builds on an earlier one, so work in order. The calculator is on, and the numbers from the prompt are already in it where that helps.

Every rule is stated in the question, and each part says how to round.

## Responsive layout maths

A container is a share of the window, cards have a width in rem, and the gaps sit only between neighbours. You find how many whole cards fit in a row and how many rows a list needs. At Expert+ a grid of equal columns and gutters gives the width of a panel in pixels and in rem, and whether an image fits.

## Page weight and load time

Images and scripts add up to a page weight in KB, and the connection speed in Mbps turns it into a load time. Later, compression shrinks the images and a time limit gives a yes or no. At Expert+ a first visit is compared with a repeat visit that uses the cache.

## Frame budget and long lists

A frame rate gives a budget in milliseconds, and a fixed cost plus a cost per item says how many items fit. Later only the rows in view are drawn (a virtualised list), and at Expert+ you find the scroll height and how many times faster that is than drawing every row.

## Colour contrast

Two relative luminances give a contrast ratio, and a 4.5 : 1 rule gives a pass or fail. Later you test a button in two states, pick the lightest text shade from a list that still passes, and at Expert+ work out how much of a dark overlay a photo needs.
