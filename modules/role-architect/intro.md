# Applied scenarios (Architect)

Expert+ questions for the **Architect** role. Each one is a single scenario with several linked answers, and each answer uses a different skill: drawing scale and area, rounding up to whole risers, windows or slabs, percentages, angles, and a final judgement against a stated rule or budget. A later part usually builds on an earlier one, so work in order. The calculator is on, and the numbers from the prompt are already in it where that helps.

Every part says how to round, and counts (risers, flights, windows, slabs, trays) are whole numbers that round **up** when you need enough, and **down** when you ask how many fit. All figures are made up for practice. Every rule is stated in the question (the 2R + G comfort range, the minimum glazing percentage, the steepest ramp, the contingency) and belongs to the exercise only: real building rules differ from place to place and must be checked with the authority that applies. This role practises the arithmetic, not the design.

## Plans at scale and cost

A drawing length at a stated scale becomes a real length, then an area and a cost. At Expert a contingency is added and compared with a budget. At Expert+ you work backwards from the budget to the largest room, and check the proposal against both the budget and the drawing sheet.

## Stairs and the 2R + G rule

A storey height gives the fewest equal risers (rounded up), the riser height and the going. Then the comfort sum 2R + G is judged against a stated range. At Expert+ the run is fixed, so you find the going, the pitch angle, and judge the stair by two rules.

## Glazing and daylight

Window areas are compared with the floor area as a percentage and with a stated minimum (and maximum at Expert). At Expert+ the floor comes from a plan at scale, you find how many windows the rule needs, and then whether a proposal also fits the wall.

## Ramps and landings

A rise and a steepest gradient of 1 in N give the length of ramp, the flights (each at most a stated length), the landings between them and the total, checked against a strip of land. At Expert+ you find how much rise a strip can give.

## Planting and paving quantities

A garden minus a patio gives the planted area, the plants, whole trays and whole slabs, and the cost. At Expert+ the paving is bought first and you find what the rest of the budget can plant.
