# Applied scenarios (Chemist)

Expert+ questions for the **Chemist** role. Each one is a single scenario with several linked answers, and each answer uses a different skill: moles, yields, titration, gas laws, percentages or costs. A later part often builds on an earlier one, so work in order. The calculator is on, and the numbers from the prompt are already in it where that helps.

Every part says how to round. If you reuse a rounded earlier answer, the later answer still comes out the same. Relative atomic masses, R = 8.31 J/(mol·K) and the 24 dm³ molar volume at RTP are always stated in the question.

## Yield and purity

A reaction run is weighed and judged. Go from mass to moles to the theoretical product, then to the percentage yield, or find an empirical formula from the masses in a sample. At Expert+ you work backwards from the product obtained to the purity of an impure sample.

## Titration and strength

An acid is neutralised by sodium hydroxide. Find the moles, the concentration, and the volume needed to dilute it, and check it against the label. At Expert+ you titrate a portion of an impure NaOH solution to find the purity of the solid.

## Gas and vessel safety

A reaction releases a gas into a sealed vessel. Find the moles, the volume at RTP and the pressure, and decide whether the vessel is safe, even when heated. At Expert+ you work back from the vessel's rating to the greatest safe charge.

## Scale-up and batch cost

A plant must deliver a target mass of product at a given yield. Find the theoretical product and the reactant to charge, cost the batch, and decide whether it makes a profit. At Expert+ you start from a budget and compare two plans.
