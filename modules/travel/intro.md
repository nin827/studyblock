# Travel and Journeys

Planning trips: how long a journey takes, how fast you need to go, what the fuel or charging costs, and how to read bus and train timetables.

| Formula | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| distance = speed × time | time in hours |
| time = distance ÷ speed | in hours; × 60 for minutes |
| speed = distance ÷ time | average speed |
| average speed = total distance ÷ total time | stops count as time |
| fuel used = km ÷ 100 × L per 100 km | the same for kWh per 100 km |

**Conventions:** speeds are in km/h and times in hours and minutes (15 min = 0.25 h). Times are typed in 24-hour format, for example `08:45`. Money is in dollars, rounded as the question says. In timetables every service takes the same time between stops, and "the next bus" means the first one leaving at or after the time you get to the stop.

| Level | What to expect |
| --- | --- |
| Easy | Whole hours, litres per 100 km, reading one bus column |
| Medium | Minutes and arrival times, trip costs, the latest or next bus |
| Hard | Average speed over two legs, the speed still needed, sharing costs, electric cars, changing from bus to train |
| Expert | Meeting and catching up, refuelling stops, electric versus petrol, a door-to-door journey |
