# Travel and Journeys: how to solve

## General

- Keep units together: km/h needs time in hours. Turn minutes into hours by dividing by 60 (45 min = 0.75 h).
- Round only at the end, to what the question asks.
- Type times as `hh:mm` in 24-hour format.

## Speed, distance and time

1. Distance = speed × time; time = distance ÷ speed; speed = distance ÷ time.
2. A time in hours, × 60, gives minutes. For an arrival time, add the journey time to the start.
3. Average speed for a whole trip = total distance ÷ total time, with any stops added to the time.
4. Speed still needed = distance left ÷ time left.
5. Two travellers towards each other close the gap at the sum of their speeds; one catching up gains at the difference of their speeds. Work out any head start first.

Example: 150 km at 60 km/h takes `150 / 60 = 2.5` h = 2 h 30 min.

**Watch out:** the average speed of 40 km/h and 60 km/h legs is not 50 km/h unless both legs take the same time.

## Fuel and trip costs

1. Fuel used = (km ÷ 100) × litres per 100 km.
2. Cost = litres × price per litre. Electric cars: kWh × price per kWh (cents ÷ 100 = dollars).
3. A round trip is twice the distance. Add tolls, then split between the people sharing.
4. Range on one tank = tank size ÷ litres per 100 km × 100.
5. Tanks needed = trip ÷ range, rounded up. You start full, so the stops are one fewer than the tanks.

Example: 250 km at 6 L per 100 km uses `2.5 × 6 = 15` L.

**Watch out:** km per litre is 100 ÷ (litres per 100 km), not the other way round.

## Timetables

1. Each column is one bus or train; each row is a stop.
2. Journey time: subtract the two times in the same column.
3. "Next bus": in your stop's row, find the first time at or after you get there.
4. "Latest bus to arrive by…": in the destination row, find the last time not after the deadline, then go up that column.
5. Changing: add the change time to your arrival at the interchange, then find the first train at or after that.
6. Waiting time = time the bus or train leaves − time you got there.

Example: reach the stop at 08:12, buses at 08:05 and 08:20: you catch the 08:20 and wait 8 min.

**Watch out:** a train leaving exactly when the change time ends can still be caught.
