# Algebra

Practice isolating an unknown and factoring simple quadratics. Some questions are multiple choice. The wrong options come from common mistakes, so read carefully.

## Linear equations

Solve `ax + b = c` for `x`: move the constant to the other side, then divide by the coefficient.

| Level | Coefficient | Answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Easy | 2 to 9 | whole number, 1 to 10 |
| Medium | -12 to 12 | whole number, may be negative |
| Hard | -15 to 15 | may be a fraction, give 2 decimals |

## Quadratic roots

Given `x² + px + q = 0`, find two numbers that **multiply to q** and **add to p**. Then factor and read off the roots. You are always asked for the **larger** root.

| Level | Roots between |
| --- | --- |
| Medium | -9 and 9 |
| Hard | -15 and 15 |
