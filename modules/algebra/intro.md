# Algebra

Practice isolating an unknown and factoring simple quadratics. Some questions are multiple choice. The wrong options come from common mistakes, so read carefully.

## Linear equations

Solve `ax + b = c` for `x`: move the constant to the other side, then divide by the coefficient.

| Level | Coefficient | Answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Easy | 2 to 9 | whole number, 1 to 10 |
| Medium | -12 to 12 | whole number, may be negative |
| Hard | -15 to 15 | may be a fraction, give 2 decimals |
| Expert | mixed | multi-step: see below |

**Expert** mixes four kinds of problem: equations with fractions and x on both sides, simultaneous equations (one with fractions), inequalities where dividing by a negative flips the sign, and word problems you set up yourself (break-even between two price plans).

## Quadratic roots

Given `x² + px + q = 0`, find two numbers that **multiply to q** and **add to p**. Then factor and read off the roots. You are always asked for the **larger** root.

| Level | Roots between |
| --- | --- |
| Medium | -9 and 9 |
| Hard | -15 and 15 |

**Expert** needs more than one step: rearrange to `ax² + bx + c = 0` first (a is not 1, and a root may be a fraction), complete the square (h, k and an irrational root), or turn an area word problem into a quadratic and reject the negative solution.
