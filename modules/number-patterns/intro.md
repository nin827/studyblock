# Patterns & Sequences

Spot the rule, then use it. Each puzzle was checked against a set of common rules (a constant step, a constant ratio, steps that grow steadily, the sum of the two before, two sequences taking turns, …): every rule that fits the terms shown gives the same answer.

## Number sequences

| Level | Rules |
| --- | --- |
| Easy | add or subtract the same amount, or multiply by 2 or 3 |
| Medium | growing steps, square numbers, add the two before, two sequences in turn |
| Hard | multiply then add, alternating operations, cubes: type the **next two** terms (or build them from blocks) |
| Expert | a missing term, a far-off term (term 20 and beyond), or the first term over a limit |

## Letter patterns & codes

Letters stand for their places in the alphabet (A = 1, B = 2, …). Some patterns wrap round from Z to A; the question says so.

At **Expert**, crack a code from one example word and use it on another. Type the word or build it from letter blocks.

## Shape patterns

Shapes are shown as symbols: filled ▲ ● ■ ◆ ★ and hollow △ ○ □ ◇ ☆, and arrows ↑ ↗ → ↘ ↓ ↙ ← ↖.

| Level | Patterns |
| --- | --- |
| Easy | a short repeat: what comes next? |
| Medium | a repeat with a shape twice, a far-off item, turning arrows, build the next three |
| Hard | shape and filling repeat on different cycles |
| Expert | far-off items (up to the 99th) and counting over the first 30–60 items |

Questions you can type or build from blocks (Hard number sequences, Expert codes) give full credit for a blocks answer at Easy and Medium, 85% at Hard and 70% at Expert.
