> [!NOTE] Made-up cases, practice only
> Every case here is invented. People are named only by their job ("the guard", "the courier"), the figures are made up, and each rule is stated in the question. This is reasoning practice, not guidance for real investigations, which need proper authority, training and your local rules.

# Applied scenarios (Investigator)

Scenario questions for the **Investigator** role, from Medium to Expert+. Each one is a single made-up case with several linked answers, and each answer uses a different skill: clock arithmetic on a timeline, deduction from statements where exactly one person lies, spotting a broken pattern or an unusual first-digit count in records, and working out how likely a flagged case is to be real. A later part usually builds on an earlier one, so work in order. The calculator is on, and the numbers from the prompt are already in it where that helps.

Times use the 24-hour clock and are typed as `HH:MM`. Money is a generic `$`, rounding is stated in each part, and every threshold or rule is given in the question.

## Timelines and alibis

Last-seen times and journey times give arrival times, then who could have been at the scene. At Expert you add the time a task needs after arriving and judge a new witness. At Expert+ you work backwards from the time of the event to the latest departure and judge two claims.

## Statements and the liar

Four or five people each make one statement, and exactly one statement is false. You find the liar and the culprit, count false statements under another assumption, and say what must follow.

## Records and patterns

Invoices that should rise by a steady step with one altered entry, then percentages. At Expert you count first digits against a stated 30% rule, and at Expert+ you work with expected counts and a band around them.

## Flags and base rates

A check flags some claims. From the true rate, the hit rate and the false-alarm rate you count real and honest flags, then the chance that a flagged claim is real. At Expert+ the false-alarm rate is hidden and you work backwards from the total flagged.
