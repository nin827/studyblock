# Applied scenarios (Physicist)

Expert+ questions for the **Physicist** role. Each one is a single scenario with several linked answers, and each answer uses a different skill: trigonometry, motion, energy, orbits, logarithms or electricity. A later part often builds on an earlier one, so work in order. The calculator is on, and the numbers from the prompt are already in it where that helps.

Every part says how to round. If you reuse a rounded earlier answer, the later answer still comes out the same. Constants such as g = 10 m/s², c = 3.0×10⁵ km/s and the specific heat of water are always stated in the question.

## Launch and slide

A block slides down a ramp, or a ball is launched at an angle. Resolve the motion with trigonometry, find the acceleration, time, speed or range, and finish with an energy check or a judgement, such as whether the ball clears a wall. At Expert+ you may work backwards from a measured sliding time to the friction on a rough ramp.

## Orbits and signals

Light and radio take time to cross the solar system. Find signal delays, use Kepler's third law to get an orbital period and speed, and decide whether a signal fits a time budget. At Expert+ you start from a star's mass and an observed period and work back to the orbit.

## Decay and safe handling

A radioactive source loses activity by half-lives. Find the decay constant, the activity left, and (with logarithms) how long to wait until it is safe to handle. At Expert+ you find the half-life from two readings and decide whether a source can be shipped.

## Heating and power

An electric heater warms water or a metal block. Work out the energy, the time, the current and the right fuse, allowing for heat losses. At Expert+ you identify an unknown metal from its heating data, plan more heating and cost the electricity.
