# Applied scenarios (Marketing Manager)

Scenario questions for the **Marketing Manager** role, from Medium to Expert+. Each one is a single made-up scenario with several linked answers, and each answer uses a different skill: percentages through a funnel, dividing spend by customers, ratios, margins, rounding up to whole people, and comparing options. A later part usually builds on an earlier one, so work in order. The calculator is on, and the numbers from the prompt are already in it where that helps.

Money is in a generic `$`. Every rule (what a stage counts, how LTV is found, what the rule threshold is, how returns diminish) is stated in the question, and each part says how to round.

## Campaign funnel

Ad views become clicks, clicks become free trials and trials become paying customers, each stage a percentage of the one before. You follow a campaign forward to its customers and cost per customer. At Expert+ you work backwards from a customer goal, rounding up at every stage, and check the ad cost against a budget.

## Acquisition cost and payback

Spend divided by new customers gives the customer acquisition cost (CAC); months of margin to earn it back gives the payback period. You compare lifetime value with CAC and apply a stated rule. At Expert+ two channels are pooled into a blended CAC, lifetime value comes from monthly churn, and you judge both the channels and the blend.

## Return on ad spend and budget split

Return on ad spend (ROAS) is revenue per dollar of ads. You compare channels, work out profit after ad spend and see what moving budget from the weaker channel to the stronger one does to revenue. At Expert+ you find the break-even ROAS and apply a diminishing-returns rule to the move.

## Promotion profit

A discount lowers the price and the profit on each unit, so more units are needed to keep the same profit. You find the sale price, the new margin and the units needed, and test a sales forecast. At Expert+ a discount competes with a free gift, and you compare their total profit with today's.
