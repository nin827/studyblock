# Inventions and Discoveries

The printing press, the aeroplane, penicillin and the World Wide Web: who was behind the breakthroughs that changed daily life, and when they happened. Each round is typed in or picked from a list.

| Level | What to expect |
| --- | --- |
| Easy | Household-name inventors and inventions |
| Medium | Key dates and the places where breakthroughs happened |
| Hard | Earlier pioneers and less familiar details |

**Note:** many inventions grew out of several people's work. Questions follow the widely accepted credit and are worded precisely, for example "first sustained, controlled, powered flight".

**Tip:** when a question asks for a year, type the digits only. Typed answers ignore capital letters and extra spaces, but spelling still counts.
