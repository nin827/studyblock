# Finance Theory

How businesses fund themselves and judge their choices: sources of finance, the trade-off between risk and return, what changes in financial ratios mean, and when to use payback, ARR or NPV. Firms and people are made up; there is no country-specific law or tax.

| Subtopic | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| Sources of finance | Internal or external, short or long term, whether ownership is given up, and picking a source by a stated rule |
| Risk and return | The risk ladder, expected return and spread of two funds, and a split portfolio at Expert |
| Ratio interpretation | Better or worse and why, working a ratio out first, and diagnosing gross versus net margin changes |
| Investment appraisal | What payback, ARR and NPV do, which suits a situation, a worked accept-or-reject decision, and projects the methods rank differently |

**Answer formats**

- **Block answers:** build the answer from one block per column, or switch to **Choose** and pick a whole row.
- **Numbers:** percentages without the `%` sign, rounded as each question says; money to the nearest dollar, without `$` or commas.

**Levels.** Easy asks one thing; Medium and Hard combine a calculation with a judgement. **Expert** needs two steps joined up: a portfolio's expected and worst-case return against a limit, margins worked out from accounts then diagnosed, and two projects where payback and NPV disagree.
