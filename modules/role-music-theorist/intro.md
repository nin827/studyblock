# Applied scenarios (Music Theorist)

Expert+ questions for the **Music Theorist** role. Each one is a single scenario with several linked answers, and each answer uses a different skill: keys and scales, chords, Roman numerals and cadences, intervals, tempo and bar lengths, ratios and transposition. A later part often builds on an earlier one, so work in order. The calculator is on, and the numbers from the prompt are already in it where that helps.

Spelled notes and chords are always picked from options, never typed. Every part says how to round, and a quarter note is one beat unless the question says otherwise. Cadence rules, tempo markings and the transposing intervals of instruments are always stated in the question. Double sharps and flats do not appear.

## Key and chords

Start from a key (or a key signature, or a chord and its degree) and find the scale, the triad on a degree, its spelling, and whether a chord belongs to the key. At Expert+ you work backwards from a chord to the key.

## Progressions and cadences

Turn Roman numerals into chord names and back, name the cadence from the last two chords, and find the key from a single chord or a signature.

## Tempo and bars

From a time signature and a tempo find the bar length, how many bars fit, how long a piece runs and whether it fits a slot. At Expert+ you work backwards from a target length to the tempo and its marking.

## Transposing parts

Move notes and whole keys up by an interval, count the new signature, and find what an instrument in B♭, A, E♭ or F really sounds. At Expert+ you work out the interval from two keys.
