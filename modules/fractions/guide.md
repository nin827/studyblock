# How to solve: Fractions

## General

- The calculator is off here: the working is the point.
- Always finish by simplifying: divide the top and bottom by their greatest common factor.
- Type fractions with no spaces around the slash, like `3/4`. A negative answer is `-7/4`. A mixed number is the whole number, a space, then the fraction: `2 1/3`.
- Use the form the question asks for. `6/8` is correct in value but marked wrong, because it isn't simplified.

## Simplifying

1. Find the greatest common factor (GCF) of the top and the bottom: the biggest number that divides both exactly.
2. Divide the top and the bottom by it.
3. Hard level, mixed number: divide the top by the bottom. The whole number is how many times it fits, and the remainder goes over the same bottom.

Example: `18/24`. The GCF is 6, so `18/24 = 3/4`.

**Watch out:** dividing by a common factor that isn't the greatest (like 2 here) leaves it unsimplified. Check again until nothing else divides both.

## Adding and subtracting

1. If the bottoms are the same, add or subtract the tops and keep the bottom.
2. If not, find the lowest common denominator (the smallest number both bottoms divide into).
3. Rewrite each fraction over it: multiply top and bottom by the same number.
4. Add or subtract the tops, keep the common bottom, then simplify.

Example: `1/3 + 1/4 = 4/12 + 3/12 = 7/12`.

**Watch out:** never add the bottoms. `1/3 + 1/4` is not `2/7`.

## Multiplying and dividing

1. Turn any mixed numbers into improper fractions: whole × bottom + top, over the bottom (`2 1/3 = 7/3`).
2. Dividing? Flip the second fraction and multiply instead (`÷ 2/5` becomes `× 5/2`).
3. Multiply the tops together and the bottoms together.
4. Simplify, and turn the result into a mixed number if the question asks for one.

Example: `3/4 ÷ 1/2 = 3/4 × 2/1 = 6/4 = 3/2`.

**Watch out:** flip only the fraction you are dividing **by**, never the first one.

## Comparing and converting

1. Fraction to decimal: divide the top by the bottom (`3/8 = 3 ÷ 8 = 0.375`). Easier when you can make the bottom 10 or 100.
2. Fraction to percent: multiply by 100 (`3/8 × 100 = 37.5`). Type the number without the % sign.
3. Decimal to fraction: count the decimal places and put the digits over 10, 100 or 1000 (`0.35 = 35/100`), then simplify. A percent is out of 100: `45% = 45/100`.
4. Comparing: put all the fractions over a common denominator and compare the tops, or turn each into a decimal.

**Watch out:** round to 2 decimal places when asked, from the third digit (`0.375` rounds to `0.38`).
