# Biology

Core facts about living things: what cells are made of, how the human body works, how characteristics are inherited, and how energy moves through ecosystems.

| Topic | Key facts |
| --- | --- |
| Cells | The nucleus holds DNA; mitochondria carry out aerobic respiration; chloroplasts carry out photosynthesis; ribosomes make proteins |
| Human body | The heart pumps blood; red blood cells carry oxygen; platelets help clotting; the kidneys filter blood |
| Genetics | Genes are sections of DNA; human body cells have 46 chromosomes; Aa × Aa gives a 3:1 phenotype ratio |
| Ecology | Producers make food by photosynthesis; consumers eat other organisms; decomposers break down dead material |

| Process | Word equation |
| --- | --- |
| Photosynthesis | carbon dioxide + water → glucose + oxygen |
| Aerobic respiration | glucose + oxygen → carbon dioxide + water |
