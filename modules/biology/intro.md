# Biology Basics

> [!WARNING] For learning only
> This isn't medical advice; for anything about your own health, ask a qualified professional.

Core facts about living things: what cells are made of, how the human body works (its bones, organs, body systems and circulation), how characteristics are inherited, and how energy moves through ecosystems.

| Topic | Key facts |
| --- | --- |
| Cells | The nucleus holds DNA; mitochondria carry out aerobic respiration; chloroplasts carry out photosynthesis; ribosomes make proteins |
| Human body | The heart pumps blood; red blood cells carry oxygen; platelets help clotting; the kidneys filter blood |
| Genetics | Genes are sections of DNA; human body cells have 46 chromosomes; Aa × Aa gives a 3:1 phenotype ratio |
| Ecology | Producers make food by photosynthesis; consumers eat other organisms; decomposers break down dead material |
| Skeleton | An adult has 206 bones; the femur (thigh) is the longest, the stapes (middle ear) the smallest |
| Organs and body systems | The liver makes bile; most food is absorbed in the small intestine; glands that release hormones form the endocrine system; blood goes heart, lungs, heart, body |

| Process | Word equation |
| --- | --- |
| Photosynthesis | carbon dioxide + water → glucose + oxygen |
| Aerobic respiration | glucose + oxygen → carbon dioxide + water |

| Level | What to expect |
| --- | --- |
| Easy | The best-known organs, organelles and terms; everyday bone names (kneecap, collarbone) |
| Medium | Blood, DNA structure, food-chain roles, anatomical bone names, heart chambers |
| Hard | Less familiar organelles, Punnett-square ratios, word equations, middle-ear bones, glands, the route through the heart |
