## General

HR scenarios chain simple steps, so work in order and keep the units straight (hours, days, people, %, $).

1. Read the stated rules first: the overtime limit and multiplier, the deduction, the leave scaling, the acceptance and pass rates, the band.
2. Write down which number each part asks for, and its rounding.
3. Whole people (starters, offers) round **up** when you need enough of them.
4. For a yes or no, find the numbers first, then compare them using the stated rule. Equal to the limit counts as within it, unless the rule says "more than" or "above".

## Payroll and overtime

Overtime hours = hours worked − the weekly limit. Gross = limit × rate + overtime hours × multiplier × rate. Net = gross × (100 − deduction)% ÷ 100. The deduction comes off gross pay.

The employer's cost starts from gross, not net: gross + on-cost % of gross, times the number of workers. Overtime share % = pay for overtime hours only ÷ combined gross × 100.

## Staff turnover and hiring

Average headcount = (start + end) ÷ 2. Turnover % = leavers ÷ average headcount × 100.

Hires needed to reach a target = target − current headcount + expected leavers (leavers must be replaced as well as the growth).

Work backwards through a funnel: people needed = people wanted ÷ the share that remain, rounded up. With probation and acceptance: starters = hires ÷ pass rate (round up), then offers = starters ÷ acceptance rate (round up).

## Leave and absence

Entitlement = full-time days × (days a week ÷ 5), and × (months employed ÷ 12) for a part-year starter, rounded to the nearest half day. Use the rounded figure afterwards: days left = entitlement − days taken. Unused days paid out = days left × daily rate.

Absence rate % = days absent ÷ (people × working days) × 100. Cover cost = days absent × cost per day.

## Pay review and budget

A raise of r% costs r% of the pay it applies to. Budget left = budget − cost.

Band midpoint = (minimum + maximum) ÷ 2. Compa-ratio = pay ÷ midpoint. Below the minimum means pay < minimum.

The top-up for each person below the minimum = minimum − their pay (zero for everyone else); add the gaps. After a blanket raise, raise every pay first and only then compare with the minimum. Total cost = raise + top-ups.
