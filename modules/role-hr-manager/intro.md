# Applied scenarios (HR Manager)

Scenario questions for the **HR Manager** role, from Medium to Expert+. Each one is a single made-up scenario with several linked answers, and each answer uses a different skill: percentages, averages, rounding up to whole people, scaling by a fraction, and a yes or no decision against a budget or limit. A later part usually builds on an earlier one, so work in order. The calculator is on, and the numbers from the prompt are already in it where that helps.

Money is a generic `$`, and every rule is stated in the question, with each part saying how to round.

## Payroll and overtime

Hours up to a weekly limit are paid at the hourly rate and the rest at a higher overtime rate. You find the gross pay, take off a flat deduction for the net pay, and at higher levels cost a crew with an on-cost or compare two workers against a weekly budget.

## Staff turnover and hiring

Turnover is the leavers as a share of the average headcount. From a headcount target you work out how many hires are needed, then, using stated acceptance and probation pass rates, how many offers must be made, rounding up at each step.

## Leave and absence

Part-time and part-year staff get a share of the full-time holiday, rounded to a half day. You work out the days left, and at higher levels the team's absence rate, the cost of cover, or the payout for unused days against a set-aside amount.

## Pay review and budget

A raise is a percentage of a payroll or of individual pay, set against a budget. With a pay band you find the midpoint, the compa-ratio (pay divided by the midpoint) and who is below the minimum, then cost the top-ups needed to bring them up to it.
