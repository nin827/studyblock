# Applied scenarios (Nutritionist)

Scenario questions for the **Nutritionist** role, from Medium to Expert+. Each one is a single made-up scenario with several linked answers, and each answer uses a different skill: energy from nutrients, scaling a portion or recipe, percentages of a stated reference intake, MET-based energy sums or fluid volumes. A later part often builds on an earlier one, so work in order. The calculator is on, and the numbers from the prompt are already in it where that helps.

Every figure is invented for practice, and every constant (4/4/9 kcal per g, 1 kcal = 4.184 kJ, 1 kg = 1 L, the reference intake) is stated in the question. Nothing here is advice about what anyone should eat, drink or do.

## Label energy

Work out energy from carbohydrate, protein and fat, scale it to a portion, and express it as a percentage of a stated reference intake. At Expert+ you work backwards from a per-portion figure to the fat content.

## Portions and recipes

Total a recipe from per-100 g values, divide by servings, and scale amounts to a different number of servings. At Expert+ you move between per-100 g and per-portion figures on a packet.

## Activity energy

Energy = MET × body mass × hours, then kcal to kJ, the average per minute, and a comparison of two sessions. At Expert+ you work back from an energy figure to the body mass.

## Fluid volumes

Turn a before and after mass change plus fluid drunk into a volume, a rate per hour, a percentage of body mass and a count of bottles. At Expert+ you work forwards from a stated rate and compare two cases.
