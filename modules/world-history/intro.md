# World History

From the pyramids to the fall of the Berlin Wall: the empires, revolutions and turning points that shaped the world. Each round is typed in or picked from a list.

| Level | What to expect |
| --- | --- |
| Easy | Famous events and dates most people meet at school |
| Medium | Key leaders, wars and turning points |
| Hard | Less familiar events, treaties and figures |

**Tip:** when a question asks for a year, type the digits only, for example `1500`. Typed answers ignore capital letters and extra spaces, but spelling still counts.
