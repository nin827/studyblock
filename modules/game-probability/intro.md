# Game Probability

Every boss drop, critical hit and loot chest runs on probability. Here you work out the numbers game designers balance and players argue about: how many tries a drop really takes, what a crit build is worth, what a chest pays on average, and how much a pity timer helps.

| Subtopic | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| Drop rates | Chance of at least one drop in n tries, average tries, tries needed for a target chance |
| Critical hits and damage | Expected damage per hit and DPS, with crits, misses and attack speed |
| Loot tables | Weighted tables: chance of an item or rarity, expected gold from a chest |
| Pity and streaks | Losing streaks, guaranteed drops on pull N, and the expected number of pulls |

**Expert** combines ideas in multi-part problems: needing several copies of a drop, working backwards from a design target to a drop rate, owning two independent drops; the chance to defeat an enemy within k hits, crit chance capped at 100%, flat armour with a 1-damage floor; the weight a new loot item needs, two-chest questions, collecting two items; soft pity, the 50/50 featured rule, and a part-used pity counter with a gem budget.

**Answer formats**

- **Percentages:** type the number without the `%` sign, rounded to 2 decimal places, like `40.13`. Answers within 0.005 count.
- **Damage, DPS, gold and expected pulls:** rounded to 2 decimal places.
- **Numbers of tries:** a whole number when the question says so.

Every try is independent unless a pity rule says otherwise, and a critical hit's multiplier is the total damage (a 2× crit deals double), not extra damage on top.
