# Applied scenarios (Insurance Underwriter)

Scenario questions for the **Insurance Underwriter** role, from Medium to Expert+. Each one is a single made-up scenario with several linked answers, each using a different skill: reading a table, multiplying factors, percentages, ratios, working loss by loss, and a yes or no or accept, refer or decline decision against a stated rule. A later part usually builds on an earlier one, so work in order. The calculator is on, and numbers from the prompt are already in it where that helps.

Money is a generic `$`, and every rule is stated in the question, with each part saying how to round.

## Premium rating

Read the bands that apply from a rating table and multiply the base premium by their factors. Higher levels add a loading and a discount in a stated order, a minimum premium, the change on last year with a cap, or two cars with a household discount and a monthly payment.

## Loss and combined ratio

From the chance of a claim and the average claim you find the expected claims cost and the loss ratio. Higher levels add the combined ratio, the underwriting result and whether the book makes a profit, or the break-even premium and the rate rise needed.

## Excess, limits and claims

The policyholder bears the excess, the insurer pays up to a limit, and co-insurance splits the rest. You work out who pays what, loss by loss, and at the top level an annual aggregate limit caps the year.

## Risk scorecard and exposure

A points table gives a score, and the score gives Accept, Refer or Decline. You also add a proposal's sum insured to the exposure already held in an area and check it against a cap, so at higher levels the final action depends on both.
