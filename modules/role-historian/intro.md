# Applied scenarios (Historian)

Expert+ questions for the **Historian** role. Each one is a single scenario with several linked answers, and every part is worked out from **facts stated in the question**: the years of well-known events, made-up documents and objects, and the rules each prompt gives. You never need to remember a date; you need to reason with it.

Years before AD 1 are written "BC" and count **down** towards AD 1. There is **no year 0**: 1 BC is followed directly by AD 1, so a span that crosses from BC into AD is one year shorter than adding the two numbers. Calendar questions use the Gregorian calendar throughout, and the opening year of every exhibition is stated.

## Dating a source

An undated document mentions events with stated years and is quoted by a later text. Work out the earliest and latest possible year of writing, how long that window is, and which event is too late to be mentioned. At Expert+ the author gives the date only relative to an event, and you check the result against everything else the document says.

## Reigns and terms

A register gives the start and end of a term of office. Find the days, the whole years completed, the weekday of the end or the date a charter lapses. At Expert+ you work back from the end date and the number of days to the start, and test it against a legal limit.

## Object labels

A museum label gives the year an object was made. Find its age, its century, its gallery under a stated rule, and how it compares with a second object. At Expert+ the label gives the year only relative to an event.

## Exhibit timelines

Order four events, find the span in years, and convert years to centimetres on a wall at a stated scale. At Expert+ you recover the scale from two dated objects and read a marker card's year off the wall.
