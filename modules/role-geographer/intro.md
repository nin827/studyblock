# Applied scenarios (Geographer)

Expert+ questions for the **Geographer** role. Each one is a single scenario with several linked answers, and each answer uses a different skill: map scale and speed, grid references and Pythagoras, bearings, contour gradients, or reading a climate table. A later part usually builds on an earlier one, so work in order. The calculator is on, and the numbers from the prompt are already in it where that helps.

Every part says how to round. All data are made up for practice. Grid references are 6-figure (3 figures easting, 3 figures northing, in tenths of a km), bearings run clockwise from grid north, clock times are 24-hour, and every classification rule (steep slope, climate bands, biome table) is stated in the question and is only for the exercise.

## Map scale and walking time

A map length becomes a real distance, then a walking time, a return time and a check against sunset. Expert+ works backwards from sunset to the latest start.

## Grid references and routes

Two 6-figure references give a distance (Pythagoras), a bearing, a feature on the line between them and a walking time. Expert+ adds a deadline and compares the direct route with a dog-leg.

## Contours and gradients

Spot heights, contour intervals and map length give the climb, the ground distance and a gradient of 1 in N, judged against a stated steepness rule. Expert+ adds the angle of slope.

## Climate tables and biomes

A twelve-month table gives the range, the total, the driest month and the mean, then a climate classification by stated bands. Expert+ recovers a missing month from the annual total.
