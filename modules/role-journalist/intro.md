# Applied scenarios (Journalist)

Scenario questions for the **Journalist** role, from Medium to Expert+. Each one is a single made-up scenario with several linked answers, and each answer uses a different skill: percentage change, rates, a margin of error, reading space and time, averages, and a yes or no verdict against a stated rule. A later part usually builds on an earlier one, so work in order. The calculator is on, and the numbers from the prompt are already in it where that helps.

Every rule is stated in the question, and each part says how to round.

## Checking a claim

A press release quotes a percentage. You work out the real percentage change, adjust for the size of the population to get a rate per 100 000, and decide whether the headline is fair, or tell a change in percentage points from a change in per cent.

## Polls and margins of error

A poll gives each side's share and a sample size. With the margin-of-error rule stated in the question you find the margin, say whether a lead is clear, work out how large a poll must be for a smaller margin, or judge whether a change between two polls is real.

## Page space and deadlines

A page holds a set number of columns of words, less headline space. You find the space left, how many words to cut, the reading time of the trimmed story, and the time in the newsroom or the latest filing time across time zones.

## Averages that mislead

A staff salary list with one very high salary pulls the mean up. You find the mean and the median, count who earns below the mean, say which one a press release used, or test how far the mean overstates the typical salary.
