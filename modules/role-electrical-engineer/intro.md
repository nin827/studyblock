# Applied scenarios (Electrical Engineer)

Expert+ questions for the **Electrical Engineer** role. Each one is a single scenario with several linked answers, and each answer uses a different skill: resistor networks, power, batteries, hex and binary, logic, sensor data or logarithms. A later part often builds on an earlier one, so work in order. The calculator is on, and the numbers from the prompt are already in it where that helps.

Every part says how to round. If you reuse a rounded earlier answer, the later answer still comes out the same. Fuse ratings, tariffs, capacities and conventions (1 kB = 1000 bytes, bits numbered from 0 at the right) are always stated in the question.

## Circuit, power and fuse

A heater or test rig is a network of resistors on a supply. Find the total resistance, the current and the power, then choose a fuse from the stated ratings. At Expert+ you work backwards from a rated power to an unknown resistor and pick a fuse and a cable together.

## Battery and energy cost

A device runs from a battery given in mAh. Convert to watt-hours, allow for the usable fraction, find the runtime and cost the charging. At Expert+ you size a battery pack from a runtime requirement and judge a supplier's offer.

## Registers and flags

A status register reads as a hex value. Convert it to binary, read named flags, count bits and follow a logic output. At Expert+ you apply AND and OR masks, or undo an XOR, and give the results in hex.

## Sensor data and storage

A sensor samples at a fixed rate with a fixed number of bits. Find the data rate, how much is stored or uploaded, or the step size and codes of an analogue-to-digital converter. At Expert+ you work backwards from a card size and a number of days to the highest sample rate, and check a design against the Nyquist rule.
