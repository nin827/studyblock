# Applied scenarios (Retail Manager)

Scenario questions for the **Retail Manager** role, from Medium to Expert+. Each one is a single made-up scenario with several linked answers, and each answer uses a different skill: conversion rates and baskets, staffing rules, markups and margins, multi-buy deals, percentages of sales and stock arithmetic. A later part usually builds on an earlier one, so work in order. The calculator is on, and the numbers from the prompt are already in it where that helps.

Every figure is made up for practice and money is in a generic `$`. The definitions are stated in each question: markup is profit as a percentage of the cost, margin is profit as a percentage of the selling price, stock figures and sales in the stock questions are at cost, shrinkage is opening stock plus purchases minus sales minus the counted closing stock, and stock turn is sales divided by average stock. Staffing rules, tolerances and targets are stated too, and each part says how to round. Real shops set their own rules.

## Footfall and staffing

Visitors and a conversion rate give purchases, and purchases times the basket give sales. A stated rule (most sales per staff member per hour) gives the number of staff, rounded up, and at Expert you cost the rota as a percentage of sales. At Expert+ you work backwards from a sales target to purchases and visitors, then judge a rota.

## Markup and margin

A cost and a markup give a selling price and the margin on it. At Expert you start from a target margin, find the price, the markup and the profit, and how many items cover a fixed cost. At Expert+ you start from a price and margin, find the cost, then test a supplier price rise against a minimum-margin policy.

## Deals and sale prices

Multi-buy deals (3 for 2, a percentage off, a half-price second or third item, a fixed price for two) are priced for a basket and compared. At Expert the shop's margin on the best deal is added. At Expert+ you work back from a total paid to the unit price, then judge two deals on price and on margin.

## Stock turn and shrinkage

Opening stock, purchases, sales and a stock count give the shrinkage in dollars and as a percentage of sales. At Expert you add the stock turn and a tolerance check. At Expert+ you start from a shrinkage percentage, work back to the counted stock, and judge the period against a tolerance and a stock-turn target.
