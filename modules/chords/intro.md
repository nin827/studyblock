# Chords

A chord is several notes played together. Here you'll spell triads and seventh chords, name chords from their notes (including inversions), and find the chords that belong to a major key. Everything is written: there's no audio.

## Building chords

Chords are stacked in **thirds**: take every other letter name. On C that gives C, E, G (and B for a seventh chord). Then choose sharps or flats to get the right sizes:

| Chord | 3rd | 5th | 7th | On C |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Major | major (4 semitones) | perfect (7) | | C, E, G |
| Minor | minor (3) | perfect (7) | | C, E♭, G |
| Diminished | minor (3) | diminished (6) | | C, E♭, G♭ |
| Augmented | major (4) | augmented (8) | | C, E, G♯ |
| Dominant 7th (C7) | major | perfect | minor (10) | C, E, G, B♭ |
| Major 7th (Cmaj7) | major | perfect | major (11) | C, E, G, B |
| Minor 7th (Cm7) | minor | perfect | minor (10) | C, E♭, G, B♭ |

Spelling matters: the D major triad is **D, F♯, A**, never D, G♭, A, because each note must use the next-but-one letter.

## Inversions

The **bass** is the lowest note. With the root in the bass, the chord is in **root position**. With the 3rd in the bass it is in **1st inversion**, and with the 5th in the bass it is in **2nd inversion**. E, G, C is C major in 1st inversion.

## Chords in a major key

The triads on each note of a major scale are **I ii iii IV V vi vii°**: capitals are major, lower case minor, and ° diminished. In C major: C, Dm, Em, F, G, Am, B°. **V7** is the dominant 7th on the 5th note.

## Expert

**Expert** questions work backwards and combine ideas: which triad or seventh chord has a given note as its 3rd, 5th or 7th; what a triad becomes when one note moves a semitone; naming seventh chords in inversion and finding a chord from its bass note; finding the key a chord belongs to; the chords of a minor key (harmonic minor); and V7 in major and minor keys.

## Answer formats

Every answer is multiple choice: notes are listed lowest first, like `D, F♯, A`. No chord needs a double sharp or flat. There's no calculator here.
