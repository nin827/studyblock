# Management

The classic textbook models of how people are managed and how firms compete. Every question names the framework it uses, and the firms and people are made up.

| Subtopic | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| Motivation (Maslow, Herzberg) | Place a workplace benefit on Maslow's hierarchy, sort Herzberg's hygiene factors from motivators, and say what improving each one does |
| Leadership and McGregor | Name a leadership style from what a manager does, match a manager's beliefs to Theory X or Theory Y, and say who decides and how messages flow |
| Organisational structure | Span of control, levels of hierarchy, chain of command, tall versus flat structures and delayering, with small calculations |
| Porter's generic strategies | Market scope (broad or narrow) and competitive advantage (lower cost or uniqueness), from descriptions or from cost and price data |

**Answer formats**

- **Block answers:** many questions ask for two or three things at once, such as *Maslow level* and *Herzberg*. Build the answer from one block per column, or switch to **Choose** and pick a whole row.
- **Numbers:** type whole numbers only. Expert cost gaps are rounded to 1 decimal place.

**Levels.** Easy asks for one classification; Medium and Hard combine two or three; **Expert** has you plan a structure backwards from a maximum span, reorganise teams against a policy, and compare two firms' strategies from data, including spotting one that is *stuck in the middle*.
