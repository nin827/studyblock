# Arithmetic

Sharpen your mental math with freshly generated problems every time. Each question is built from a seed, so it can be replayed exactly.

| Subtopic | Easy | Medium | Hard |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Addition | 1–20 | 10–999 | 1,000–99,999 |
| Subtraction | 1–20, never negative | 10–999, never negative | 100–9,999, may be negative |
| Multiplication | 1–10 × 1–10 | 11–99 × 2–12 | 100–999 × 11–99 |
| Division | divisor 1–10, whole answer | divisor 2–25, whole answer | 100–9,999 ÷ 3–99, 2 decimals |

**Tip:** on hard division, round to 2 decimal places (answers within 0.005 are accepted).
