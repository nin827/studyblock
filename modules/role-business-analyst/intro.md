# Business Analyst scenarios

Expert+ questions for the Business Analyst role. Each one is a single business case with four linked parts, and each part uses a different skill. Later parts usually build on an earlier answer, so work them in order.

Every figure is made up, and every rule you need (thresholds, a bank's limits, a board's tests) is stated in the question.

## Sales review

Write an SQL query over a **sales** table to find the product under review. Then work out how fast its market grew, place it in the **Boston matrix** with its relative market share, and pick a strategy (build, hold, harvest or divest).

## Loan application review

From a balance sheet extract, work out a **liquidity ratio** (current or acid test) and **gearing**, say whether one of them improved on last year, and apply the bank's lending limits.

## Investment case

Turn units and contribution per unit into cash flows. Then find the **payback**, the **NPV** from discount factors, and how far sales could fall before the NPV reaches zero, and give the board's decision.

## Launch and pricing plan

Size a **target segment** from population shares, place the category in the **product life cycle**, compare **skimming** and **penetration** prices by contribution, and choose the launch plan.
