# Balancing & Progression

Why does level 30 take so much longer than level 3? How much does armour really help, and is +20% then +20% the same as +40%? Game designers answer these with a handful of formulas. Here you use them the way a designer would, to tune levelling, combat and the in-game economy.

| Subtopic | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| XP curves | XP for one level-up, total XP to reach a level, and what level a player is on, for linear, quadratic and growing (geometric) curves |
| Damage and armour | Flat and percentage reduction, armour formulas and penetration, hits to defeat an enemy and time to kill |
| Stat scaling | Stats that grow each level, and bonuses that stack additively or multiplicatively |
| Game economy | Gold per hour, runs needed to afford an item, sinks and faucets, and how fast gold piles up |

**Every formula is given in the question**, so read it carefully: a game might subtract flat armour first, or say the first hit lands at time 0.

**Answer formats**

- **Whole numbers** for levels, hits, runs, days and exact XP or gold.
- **Rounded answers:** the question says how, either to the nearest whole number or to 2 decimal places (answers within 0.005 count).
- **Changes** that can go down are typed with a minus sign.
- XP(k) always means the XP to go from level k to level k + 1, starting at level 1 with 0 XP.
