# Applied scenarios (Event Planner)

Scenario questions for the **Event Planner** role, from Medium to Expert+. Each one is a single made-up event with several linked answers, and each answer uses a different skill: dividing guests into whole tables, adding up floor areas, counting along a clock, working out percentages of a budget, and a yes or no decision. A later part usually builds on an earlier one, so work in order. The calculator is on, and the numbers from the prompt are already in it where that helps.

Money is in a generic `$`. Times are in 24-hour `HH:MM`, and every rule is stated in the question, with each part saying how to round.

## Seating and floor plan

Guests are divided into whole round tables (a part-table still counts), each table takes a stated area of floor, and the total is compared with the room. Later there is a head table, and at Expert+ a mix of tables of 10 and 8 with a dance floor.

## The run-of-show

A programme of timed items with buffers between them has a total length and a finish time. You also count back from a curfew for the latest start, work out set-up before the doors, and at Expert+ cope with speeches that overrun.

## Budget and ticket price

Per-guest and fixed costs give a total, then contingency and a sponsor's share change the budget. You find the lowest whole-dollar ticket price that covers the rest, and at Expert+ compare two caterers, one with a minimum charge.

## Shuttles and coaches

Guests fill whole coaches or minibuses, a shuttle loop has a round-trip time, and several trips follow one another. You find the last arrival, and at Expert+ how many buses are needed to get everyone there by a deadline, and what they cost.
