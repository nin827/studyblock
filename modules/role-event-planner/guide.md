## General

Event scenarios chain simple steps, so work in order and keep the units straight (guests, tables, m², minutes, $, HH:MM).

1. Read the stated rules first: table areas, buffers, overruns, minimum charges, loading times.
2. Write down which number each part asks for, and its rounding.
3. Whole tables, coaches and buses round **up** when you need enough; "how many fit" rounds **down**.
4. For a yes or no, find the numbers first, then compare them using the stated rule.
5. For a clock time, work in minutes and convert back to `HH:MM` at the end (60 minutes to an hour).

## Seating and floor plan

Tables = guests ÷ seats per table, rounded up. Empty seats = tables × seats − guests. Floor area needed = tables × area per table (plus a head table's or dance floor's own area). Room area = width × length. It fits if the area needed is no more than the room's.

For the capacity, take any head table's area off the room first, then count whole tables in what is left (round down), and add their seats and the head table's guests.

With tables of 10 and 8: as many full tables of 10 as possible, then the leftover guests ÷ 8 rounded up. A dance floor is its area per guest × guests.

## The run-of-show

Total length = item lengths added + one buffer for each gap (one fewer than the items). Finish = start + total. Latest start = curfew − total.

Set-up and doors work backwards from the start: doors = start − lead time; set-up start = doors − set-up time. Pack-down works forwards: finish + pack-down time, compared with the time the venue must be empty.

Speeches that run 10% over add 10% of their own planned length each; buffers do not change. Minutes to cut = actual finish − curfew.

## Budget and ticket price

Total cost = cost per guest × guests + fixed costs. Contingency = its % of the total; budget = total + contingency. A sponsor covers its % of the budget; tickets must raise the rest. Break-even ticket = the amount to raise ÷ guests, rounded **up** to a whole dollar.

A minimum charge means you pay whichever is greater: per-head total or the minimum. A flat set-up fee is added to the per-head total. Pick the cheaper quote, then build the budget from it.

## Shuttles and coaches

Coaches = guests ÷ seats, rounded up; empty seats = coaches × seats − guests. A shuttle's round trip (departure to departure) = drive there + unload + drive back + load. Trips = guests ÷ capacity, rounded up.

The last departure is at the first departure + (trips − 1) × round trip, and the last arrival adds one drive. With several buses, share the trips out (round up for the busiest bus).

For a deadline: the latest departure is the deadline − the one-way drive. Count departures at the first time and every round trip after, up to that latest one; buses needed = trips ÷ trips per bus, rounded up. Cost = buses × fee.
