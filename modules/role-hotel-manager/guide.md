## General

Hotel scenarios chain simple steps, so work in order and keep the counts straight (rooms available, rooms sold, guests, minutes, $).

1. Read the stated rules first: no-show rate, walk cost, cleaning minutes, shift length, discount level, service charge, minimum profit.
2. Check what each percentage is "of": rooms available, rooms sold, bookings, or the quote.
3. Round only as each part asks. Whole people and rooms needed round up; things that fit into spare time or a limit round down.
4. For a verdict, find the number first, then compare it with the stated rule.

## Occupancy, ADR and RevPAR

Occupancy % = rooms sold ÷ rooms available × 100. ADR = room revenue ÷ rooms sold. RevPAR = room revenue ÷ rooms available, which also equals occupancy (as a fraction) × ADR.

Room revenue with several room types is the sum of rooms sold × rate for each type. To reach a target RevPAR at a given rate, revenue needed = target × rooms available, and rooms to sell = revenue needed ÷ rate, rounded up. A percentage cut gives new rate = rate × (100 − cut) ÷ 100. To compare plans, work out each one's RevPAR in full, then compare.

## Overbooking and walk costs

Expected no-shows = bookings × no-show %. Guests who arrive = bookings − no-shows. Guests walked = arrivals − rooms (when positive).

Net gain from overbooking = revenue with overbooking − revenue with only the rooms booked. Overbooking fills the rooms the base plan loses to no-shows, so gain = (rooms the base plan loses) × room revenue − walked × walk cost. It breaks even when walked × walk cost equals that extra revenue.

To find the most bookings a walk-cost limit allows: guests walked at most = limit ÷ walk cost, rounded down; most arrivals = rooms + that; most bookings = arrivals ÷ the share who show up, rounded down.

## Housekeeping staffing

Total minutes = check-outs × minutes each + stay-overs × minutes each (+ vacant checks × minutes each). Attendants = total minutes ÷ minutes per attendant, rounded up. Labour cost per room = attendants × cost per shift ÷ rooms cleaned.

Idle minutes = attendants × shift − minutes needed. Extra rooms that fit = idle ÷ minutes per room, rounded down. Occupied rooms = rooms × occupancy %; check-outs are a percentage of the occupied rooms and the rest are stay-overs; vacant rooms = rooms − occupied. A roster is enough when its attendants × shift covers the minutes needed.

## Event quotes

Quote = guests × price per head + room hire. Deposit = quote × deposit %. Profit = quote − (cost per guest × guests + fixed costs). Margin % = profit ÷ quote × 100.

Break-even: each guest adds price − cost per guest, and the room hire offsets the fixed costs, so guests = (fixed costs − hire) ÷ (price − cost), rounded up.

A group discount applies only when the group reaches the stated size: price = price × (100 − discount) ÷ 100. A service charge is added to the client's bill (bill = income × (100 + charge) ÷ 100) but goes to staff, so it is not part of the hotel's income or profit.
