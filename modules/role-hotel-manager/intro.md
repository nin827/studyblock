# Applied scenarios (Hotel Manager)

Scenario questions for the **Hotel Manager** role, from Medium to Expert+. Each one is a single made-up hotel scenario with several linked answers, and each answer uses a different skill: percentages of rooms, averages, rounding whole people or rooms up, cost against revenue and a Yes/No decision against a stated rule. A later part sometimes builds on an earlier one, so work in order. The calculator is on, and the numbers from the prompt are already in it where that helps.

Money is in a generic `$`. Every rule (the no-show rate, walk cost, cleaning minutes, shift length, discount level, service charge, minimum profit) is stated in the question, and each part says how to round.

## Occupancy, ADR and RevPAR

Work out occupancy, the average daily rate and revenue per available room from one night's rooms and revenue. At Expert there are two room types, and at Expert+ you find the rooms needed to reach a target RevPAR and judge a price-cut promotion against last night and the target.

## Overbooking and walk costs

A hotel books more rooms than it has because some guests never arrive. You find the expected no-shows, the guests left without a room and the revenue gained or lost compared with booking only the rooms. At Expert you find the walk cost at which overbooking stops paying, and at Expert+ the most bookings a walk-cost limit allows.

## Housekeeping staffing

Check-out rooms and stay-over rooms take different cleaning times. You add up the minutes, work out how many attendants are needed (whole people, rounded up) and the labour cost per room. At Expert the unused time is turned into extra rooms, and at Expert+ the rooms come from an occupancy percentage and a roster is checked.

## Event quotes

A banquet quote is guests times price per head plus room hire. You add a deposit, then profit and margin against the hotel's own costs, the fewest guests that break even and, at Expert+, a group discount, a service charge on the client's bill and a minimum-profit decision.
